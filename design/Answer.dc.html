“My landlord changed the locks while I was away”
Punjab · as at today · plain speech
You have six months This remedy is time-barred after that. Counted from the day you were dispossessed.
You can ask a civil court to put you back in possession, and you do not have to prove you own the property — only that you were in possession and were removed without your consent.
s.9 Specific Relief Act
Changing the locks to force you out can also be a criminal offence, separate from the civil claim.
s.441 Penal Code partly supported

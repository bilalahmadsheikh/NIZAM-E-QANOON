Four of these are not styling decisions. The citation chip, the status banner, the scope bar and the disclaimer are the visible half of guarantees the architecture makes — a redesign may restyle them but may not remove them.

Citation chip
s.9 Specific Relief Act partly supported
Takes a resolved citation object, never strings to join. It cannot render without a real identity, so a fabricated section number has nowhere to come from. Tapping always opens the source.
Status banner
Repealed 1 July 2009
Rights accrued before this date are saved by s.32(2).
Sits above the text it qualifies, never below. Cannot be collapsed for repealed or not-in-force, and carries the savings clause rather than implying total loss.
Scope bar
Punjab · as at 28 Aug 2026 · plain speech
Permanent. An answer is only true within a jurisdiction and a date, so both stay on screen rather than hiding in settings.
Disclaimer
Legal information, not advice. Consult an advocate for your matter.
Structural, not dismissible, on every generated surface. Quiet enough to live with and present enough to mean something.

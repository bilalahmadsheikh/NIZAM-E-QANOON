Nizam-e-Qanoon · Design System 01
Colour
Jade rather than the navy-and-gold every legal product reaches for. It carries the green of Pakistani judicial stamp paper without being flag green, and it reads as valid, which is the meaning the interface needs most often. One accent, one oxide for danger, one ochre for caution — nothing else.

One tint, not a palette
Jade does every interactive job — links, buttons, selection, tab bar. iOS apps that feel calm use exactly one tint colour and let the content carry everything else.
Status colour is reserved
Oxide and ochre mean one thing each and are never borrowed for decoration. If a fifth state appears, it gets a shape, not a hue.
Dark is designed, not flipped
Jade gains lightness and keeps chroma so it stays the same colour. An algorithmic invert would turn it grey and lose the identity.

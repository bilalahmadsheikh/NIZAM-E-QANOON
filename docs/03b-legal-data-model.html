§1Linkage
model

Law is a graph with typed edges

A generic document store treats a statute as text with a title. A legal database has to treat it as a node in a graph where the edge type carries legal meaning — because “notwithstanding section 5” and “subject to section 5” point at the same provision and mean opposite things.

1.1The fourteen edge types

“RAG use” states what the edge does at retrieval time. Expand pulls extra context; Guard prevents a wrong answer; Rank reorders.
EdgeLegal meaningRAG use
containsStructural parent to childExpand Never return a clause without its section; never a proviso without what it qualifies
amendsInstrument closes one version and opens anotherGuard Selects the right text for as_of; surfaces the amendment chain
repealsInstrument ends the life of anotherGuard Repealed law is never operative for a present-day question
savesA repeal that preserves rights accrued, or proceedings begun, under the old lawGuard The reason a repealed act can still be the correct answer. Without this edge, repeal looks total when it is not
commencesA notification brings a section into force, often years after enactment and section by sectionGuard An enacted but uncommenced section is not law yet
empowersA section authorises rules, regulations or an SRO to be made under itExpand The highest-value edge. The Act says “as prescribed”; the prescription lives in delegated legislation. Retrieving one without the other returns half an answer
definesA term is given a meaning, scoped to an act, a part or a single sectionExpand Definitions inject deterministically when a retrieved provision uses a defined term
defaults_fromGeneral interpretation statutes supply meanings not displaced by the specific actExpand Fallback when a term is used but not locally defined
notwithstandingNon-obstante: this provision prevails over the one namedRank Order conflicting provisions correctly and flag the conflict rather than silently picking one
subject_toThis provision yields to the one namedRank The inverse. Both must be retrieved together or the answer inverts
refers_toA plain internal or external cross-referenceExpand Graph expansion at stage 1 of retrieval
interpreted_byJudicial treatment: applies, distinguishes, doubts, overrulesExpand The citator, and evidence that a reading is still good law
invalidated_byA superior court has struck down or declared a provision of no effectGuard A struck-down provision still appears in the printed statute. Serving it as operative is the worst failure available
attractsLinks a provision to a structured schedule row — offence attributes, court fee, stamp duty, limitation periodGuard Answered by SQL, exactly, with no model in the path

As-built structural release boundary

Migration 0035 makes parser uncertainty a first-class legal-model fact. Migration 0036 makes corrective decisions fail closed and records exact identity evidence. Migration 0037 blocks a tree while its printed contents promises an unresolved provision. Migration 0038 records source-evidenced internal boundaries; migration 0040 materialises each reviewed primary or embedded legal expression with a stable ordinal and immutable source-block span. Application graph reads use the release views. On 10 September 2026 this publishes 3,720 instruments and 300,137 provisions while retaining all 2,312 unresolved S7 decisions and 1,988 canonical contents gaps for review; S10 is clear.

Why empowers deserves special attention

Pakistani statutes delegate constantly: an act sets a framework and says the fee, the form, the time limit or the procedure shall be “as prescribed” or “as may be notified”. The operative number then lives in Rules or an SRO that may have been amended a dozen times since.

A retrieval system that returns only the parent Act gives a confidently incomplete answer — and it will read as authoritative because the Act is authoritative. Modelling the empowers edge means the assembler can always ask: does this section delegate, and if so is the current delegated instrument in the context? If it delegates and the delegated instrument is missing from the corpus, that is a stated gap, not a silent one.

§2Faceted
taxonomy

Categorise on nine axes, not one

Every legal database that fails does so by choosing a single category tree. Law does not have one. An instrument sits somewhere on every axis simultaneously and independently — and each axis is a different retrieval filter serving a different question.

2.1What each facet is for

FacetCardinalityQuery it answersWhere the values come from
subject_matter1–3“Show me tenancy law” · narrows dense retrieval before rankingSeeded from the Pakistan Code’s own categories, then extended. Assigned by rule where the short title is decisive, by classifier otherwise, human-reviewed
instrument_type1“Only primary legislation” · distinguishes an Act from the SRO made under itDeterministic from the source
jurisdiction1The hardest filter in the system. Punjab tenancy law is not Sindh tenancy law, and returning the wrong province is a wrong answerDeterministic from the source
competence1Whether a provincial enactment can validly cover a subject at all — and what the 18th Amendment movedConstitution’s legislative lists, curated once
doctrinal_role1–3“What are my rights” wants substantive; “how do I file” wants procedural. Routes intent to the right corpus sliceRule plus review
legal_origin1–2Explains interpretive tradition; flags where Federal Shariat Court jurisdiction is liveCurated by era and subject
life_stage1Every read. An instrument may be enacted, commenced, repealed, spent, lapsed or invalidated, and only one of those is operativeDerived from the temporal model in document 03
citizen_situation0–4The bridge from “my landlord locked me out” to the Specific Relief Act. See §5Hand-curated. The most valuable and most labour-intensive facet
forum0–3Which court or tribunal hears a matter — drives the forum finder and filters precedent by court seniorityFrom the act’s own jurisdiction clauses

Facets apply at two levels, and that matters

Most facets attach to the instrument — jurisdiction, type, competence, origin. But subject_matter, doctrinal_role and citizen_situation also attach at provision level, because a single act is rarely homogeneous: the Pakistan Penal Code is penal throughout, but the Companies Act contains substantive, procedural and penal provisions in the same chapter. Filtering at instrument granularity when the query is about a section throws away precision the corpus already has.

§3Pakistan-
specific

Constructs a generic legal schema will miss

These are the places where importing a design built for another jurisdiction produces a database that is subtly, confidently wrong about Pakistani law.

ConstructWhy it breaks a naive modelSchema response
Section-level commencement Many acts commence “on such date as the Government may by notification appoint” — and different sections can be appointed on different dates, sometimes years apart. An instrument-level commenced_on is therefore not enough commencement rows keyed to provision, with the notification that appointed the date. A provision with no commencement row and an act with none is enacted but not in force
Territorial extent, which changes “Extends to the whole of Pakistan except…” is a live, temporal attribute, not boilerplate. The 2018 merger of the Federally Administered Tribal Areas into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa changed where a large body of law applies extent as a temporal relation: instrument × territory × validity range. Retrieval filters on it exactly as it filters on as_of
Repeal with savings A repealing act commonly preserves rights accrued, liabilities incurred, and proceedings begun under the repealed law. Treating repeal as deletion makes the system wrong about every matter that started before it The saves edge, pointing from the repealing provision to what it preserves, with the text of the savings clause. Repealed law stays queryable and is returned with its savings clause attached
Federal Shariat Court repugnancy Under Article 203-D the Court may declare a provision repugnant to the Injunctions of Islam, with effect from a stated date unless amended before it. The provision remains printed in the statute book while ceasing to have effect A distinct life_stage value and an invalidated_by edge carrying the declaring judgment, the effective date, and whether the declaration is under appeal. Never inferable from the statute text alone
Ultra vires declarations A superior court can strike down a provision as beyond legislative competence or unconstitutional. The printed text does not change Same edge, different ground. Both are guards on every read
Post-18th-Amendment competence The abolition of the Concurrent Legislative List in 2010 devolved subjects to the provinces. Federal acts on devolved subjects may continue until replaced — so both a federal and a provincial act can appear valid for the same subject competence facet plus a superseded_by relation across jurisdictions. When both surface, present both with their status rather than silently choosing
Default definitions General interpretation statutes supply meanings to every enactment unless the specific act displaces them. A term used but not defined locally is not undefined The defaults_from edge, resolved only after a local definition lookup misses. Scope order: section → part → act → general
Non-obstante precedence “Notwithstanding” and “subject to” encode which provision prevails. Flattened into an untyped link, the answer to a conflict is lost Two directed edge types with opposite polarity, used at ranking time to order and to flag
Bilingual authenticity Where an Urdu text exists, which language version governs is a legal question, not a presentation choice authentic_language on the instrument. The client shows the authentic text verbatim and labels the other as translation — never the reverse

The two edges that cannot be extracted from statute text

invalidated_by and commences both live outside the document they modify — one in a judgment, one in a gazette notification. No amount of parsing the Act will find them, which means a corpus built only from statute PDFs will confidently serve provisions that a court has struck down or that never came into force.

Practically: until the judgment and gazette pipelines land, those two facets carry a coverage flag and the UI states that invalidation status is not yet tracked. An unknown that is declared is a limitation; an unknown that is hidden is a defect.

§4Schedules
as tables

The schedules are already tables — store them as tables

Several of the most-asked legal questions in Pakistan are answered by a schedule that is literally printed as a grid. Parsed into rows, they turn a retrieval-plus-generation problem into a SELECT — exact, instant, free, and impossible to hallucinate.

Printed containers are not interchangeable. The implemented grammar retains schedule, form, appendix, annexure and order as distinct provision kinds; CPC Rules remain children of their printed Orders. A container may later own a normalized cell table, but its source blocks, printed label, page range and citation lineage remain canonical. Repeated numbers are never flattened into schedule rows merely to remove a collision: unresolved cases stay in the S7 queue.

Estimated row counts. Each of these is a bounded, one-time data-entry task with permanent value, and each removes a class of question from the model entirely.
Source scheduleRowsAnswers, exactlyWhy the model must not do it
CrPC Schedule II — offence attributes~300 Is this offence cognizable? Bailable? Compoundable? Which court tries it? Can the police arrest without warrant? These are the questions with the most direct consequences for a person’s liberty, and the answer is a grid cell. Generating it is indefensible when reading it is trivial
Limitation Act 1908 — Schedule~180 How long do I have to file? From what date does the clock run? Missing a limitation period is one of the most common and most irreversible self-representation errors. A near-miss answer is worse than none
Court Fees Act 1870 — Schedules I & II~110
× province		 What will it cost to file this suit at this value? Arithmetic over a rate table, per province, versioned by amendment date
Stamp Act 1899 — Schedule I~65
× province		 What stamp duty does this instrument attract? Drives the document factory’s duty computation. Provincial amendments diverge sharply
Civil courts pecuniary limits~40 Which civil court has jurisdiction at this suit value, in this district? The forum finder. Wrong forum means a plaint returned and time lost 
CREATE TABLE offence_attribute (            -- CrPC Schedule II, one row per offence
  provision_id   uuid PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES provision(id),
  cognizable     boolean NOT NULL,
  warrant        boolean NOT NULL,          -- arrest without warrant permitted?
  bailable       boolean NOT NULL,
  compoundable   text NOT NULL,             -- none | by_victim | with_court_permission
  triable_by     text NOT NULL,             -- magistrate class, sessions, special court
  punish_min     interval, punish_max interval,
  fine_note      text,
  validity       daterange NOT NULL,       -- the Schedule is itself amended
  source_note    text NOT NULL
);

CREATE TABLE limitation_article (
  id             uuid PRIMARY KEY,
  article_no     text NOT NULL,
  description    text NOT NULL,             -- "for possession of immoveable property…"
  period         interval NOT NULL,
  runs_from      text NOT NULL,             -- the trigger event, verbatim from the Schedule
  jurisdiction   jurisdiction NOT NULL,
  validity       daterange NOT NULL
);

CREATE TABLE provision_limitation (         -- the "attracts" edge, made concrete
  provision_id   uuid REFERENCES provision(id),
  article_id     uuid REFERENCES limitation_article(id),
  confidence     real NOT NULL,
  verified_by    text,
  PRIMARY KEY (provision_id, article_id)
);

The rule that makes these safe

Every row carries validity and source_note. Schedules are amended like anything else, and a fee table without a validity range will silently answer 2019 questions with 2026 rates. And the calculator surfaces the article it applied, its period, and its trigger — so a user or an advocate can check the reasoning rather than trusting a number.

These tables are expert-entered and expert-verified, never model-extracted. The value here is exactness; a 97%-accurate limitation table is not 97% as good as a correct one, it is a liability.

§5Situation
bridge

The citizen taxonomy is a separate artifact

A doctrinal taxonomy serves lawyers. It does not help someone who types مالک مکان نے تالا لگا دیا or malik makan ne tala laga diya. The bridge between lived situations and legal provisions is hand-built, and it is the single highest-leverage data asset in the product.

CREATE TABLE situation (
  id           uuid PRIMARY KEY,
  slug         text NOT NULL UNIQUE,        -- eviction_without_process
  title_en     text NOT NULL,               -- "Locked out by a landlord"
  title_ur     text NOT NULL,
  summary_en   text, summary_ur text,
  facet_situation text NOT NULL,            -- housing | work | family | money | crime …
  urgency      text                            -- has_deadline | time_sensitive | routine
);

CREATE TABLE situation_alias (               -- how people actually say it
  situation_id uuid REFERENCES situation(id),
  phrase       text NOT NULL,
  script       text NOT NULL,               -- latin | arabic | roman_urdu
  PRIMARY KEY (situation_id, phrase)
);

CREATE TABLE situation_provision (
  situation_id uuid REFERENCES situation(id),
  provision_id uuid REFERENCES provision(id),
  jurisdiction jurisdiction NOT NULL,       -- the same situation maps differently per province
  role         text NOT NULL,               -- primary | supporting | penal | procedural | remedy
  note         text,
  verified_by  text NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (situation_id, provision_id, jurisdiction)
);

Roughly 200 situations covers the overwhelming majority of citizen need, and they are enumerable in advance: housing, unpaid wages, wrongful dismissal, divorce and khula, child custody, inheritance shares, property transfer and registration, refusal to register an FIR, bail, defective goods, dishonoured cheque, land encroachment, workplace harassment, domestic violence, succession certificates, nikah registration. Each takes an advocate perhaps twenty minutes to map and review.

§6Postgres
schema

Linkage and facets in Postgres

These tables sit alongside the core schema in document 03. One edge table with a typed discriminator, one facet table per level, and the temporal relations that the naive model gets wrong.

CREATE TYPE edge_kind AS ENUM (
  'contains','amends','repeals','saves','commences','empowers','defines',
  'defaults_from','notwithstanding','subject_to','refers_to',
  'interpreted_by','invalidated_by','attracts');

CREATE TABLE legal_edge (
  id           bigint GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY PRIMARY KEY,
  kind         edge_kind NOT NULL,
  src_provision uuid REFERENCES provision(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  src_instrument uuid REFERENCES instrument(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  dst_provision uuid REFERENCES provision(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  dst_instrument uuid REFERENCES instrument(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  dst_judgment  uuid,
  dst_schedule  uuid,
  validity     daterange NOT NULL DEFAULT '(,)',   -- edges are temporal too
  raw_text     text,                              -- the clause that created the edge
  confidence   real NOT NULL DEFAULT 1.0,
  method       text NOT NULL,                    -- grammar | model | human
  verified_by  text,
  CONSTRAINT ck_one_src CHECK (num_nonnulls(src_provision, src_instrument) = 1),
  CONSTRAINT ck_one_dst CHECK (
    num_nonnulls(dst_provision, dst_instrument, dst_judgment, dst_schedule) = 1)
);

CREATE INDEX ix_edge_out ON legal_edge (src_provision, kind);
CREATE INDEX ix_edge_in  ON legal_edge (dst_provision, kind);
CREATE INDEX ix_edge_kind ON legal_edge (kind) WHERE kind IN
  ('invalidated_by','repeals','commences');   -- partial: the guards are read on every query

6.1Facets and the temporal relations

CREATE TABLE instrument_facet (
  instrument_id uuid REFERENCES instrument(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  facet         text NOT NULL,       -- subject_matter | doctrinal_role | legal_origin …
  value         text NOT NULL,
  taxonomy_ver  integer NOT NULL,    -- see §8; a value means nothing without its version
  method        text NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (instrument_id, facet, value)
);

CREATE TABLE provision_facet (       -- three facets also apply at provision level
  provision_id uuid REFERENCES provision(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  facet        text NOT NULL CHECK (facet IN
                 ('subject_matter','doctrinal_role','citizen_situation')),
  value        text NOT NULL,
  taxonomy_ver integer NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (provision_id, facet, value)
);

CREATE TABLE commencement (           -- section-level, and often years after enactment
  provision_id     uuid REFERENCES provision(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  commenced_on     date NOT NULL,
  by_instrument_id uuid REFERENCES instrument(id),   -- the appointing notification
  PRIMARY KEY (provision_id)
);

CREATE TABLE extent (                 -- where an instrument applies, and when it changed
  instrument_id uuid REFERENCES instrument(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  territory     text NOT NULL,
  included      boolean NOT NULL,     -- "except the Tribal Areas" is an exclusion row
  validity      daterange NOT NULL,
  source_note   text NOT NULL,
  EXCLUDE USING gist (instrument_id WITH =, territory WITH =, validity WITH &&)
);

6.2The guard, as one query

-- every read passes through this. It is a view so it cannot be forgotten.
CREATE VIEW operative_provision AS
SELECT p.*, v.text_en, v.validity
FROM provision p
JOIN instrument i ON i.id = p.instrument_id AND i.published
JOIN provision_version v ON v.provision_id = p.id
WHERE v.validity @> current_setting('nzq.as_of')::date
  AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM commencement c
              WHERE c.provision_id = p.id
                AND c.commenced_on <= current_setting('nzq.as_of')::date)
  AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM legal_edge e
                  WHERE e.dst_provision = p.id
                    AND e.kind = 'invalidated_by'
                    AND e.validity @> current_setting('nzq.as_of')::date)
  AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM extent x
              WHERE x.instrument_id = i.id AND x.included
                AND x.territory = current_setting('nzq.territory')
                AND x.validity @> current_setting('nzq.as_of')::date);

Retrieval reads operative_provision, never provision. A provision that is repealed, uncommenced, judicially invalidated, or not in force in the user’s territory cannot appear in a result set — not because the retrieval code remembered to filter, but because the only view it is granted access to has already done so.

§7SQLite
projection

The pack is a projection, not a second design

Same identities, same semantics, fewer enforcement mechanisms — because the invariants were enforced upstream by the engine that has them. The pack is read-only and server-built, which is what makes that safe.

-- pack schema, abridged. Same identities as the server; no uuid type, so TEXT.

CREATE TABLE provision (
  id            TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
  instrument_id TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES instrument(id),
  path          TEXT NOT NULL,          -- 'fed/act/1860_45/ch_XVI/s_302/cl_c'
  kind          TEXT NOT NULL CHECK(kind IN('section','clause','proviso', …)),
  label         TEXT NOT NULL, heading TEXT, ordinal INTEGER NOT NULL,
  -- facets denormalised onto the row: the device does no analytics
  subject       TEXT, doctrinal TEXT, situation TEXT,
  -- guard columns precomputed at build time; the device never recomputes them
  in_force      INTEGER NOT NULL,       -- already accounts for repeal, commencement,
  invalidated   INTEGER NOT NULL        -- invalidation and extent, at pack build date
);
CREATE INDEX ix_prov_path ON provision(path);        -- prefix LIKE uses this
CREATE INDEX ix_prov_instr ON provision(instrument_id, ordinal);

CREATE TABLE provision_text (
  provision_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES provision(id),
  valid_from   TEXT NOT NULL,           -- ISO-8601; sorts and compares as text
  valid_to     TEXT,                    -- NULL = current
  text_en      TEXT, text_ur TEXT
);

CREATE VIRTUAL TABLE fts USING fts5(
  text_en, text_ur, heading,
  content='',                          -- contentless: no second copy of the corpus
  tokenize="unicode61 remove_diacritics 2"
);

CREATE VIRTUAL TABLE vec USING vec0(       -- optional semantic tier only
  provision_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
  embedding float[384]
);

CREATE TABLE legal_edge (               -- six kinds; the rest need judgments or gazettes
  kind TEXT NOT NULL CHECK(kind IN
    ('contains','defines','refers_to','empowers','attracts','saves')),
  src  TEXT NOT NULL, dst TEXT NOT NULL, note TEXT,
  PRIMARY KEY(kind, src, dst)
) WITHOUT ROWID;

The guard columns are the whole trick

The server evaluates repeal, commencement, invalidation and territorial extent at pack build time and writes the answer into two integer columns. The device then does a single indexed comparison instead of four correlated subqueries against tables it does not carry.

The cost is that those columns are as at the pack build date, which is exactly why every offline provision view is stamped with the pack version. And it is why a patch that changes a provision’s status must rewrite these columns — the same invalidation discipline the answer tier already uses.

§8Governance

The taxonomy is versioned data, not configuration

Facet values will be wrong at first and will improve. If a stored value cannot be traced to the vocabulary version that produced it, every reclassification silently invalidates every measurement taken before it.

CREATE TABLE taxonomy_version (
  version     integer PRIMARY KEY,
  facet       text NOT NULL,
  values      jsonb NOT NULL,        -- the permitted vocabulary at this version
  released_on date NOT NULL,
  rationale   text NOT NULL
);

8.1What ships first

  1. Deterministic facets and the guard view — jurisdiction, type, life stage. They cost nothing and prevent the worst failures.
  2. The contains, refers_to and defines edges — extractable from the statute text we already have, and they carry most of the retrieval-expansion value.
  3. Schedule tables — bounded expert data entry, immediate exact answers, no model in the path.
  4. The situation taxonomy — roughly 70 advocate-hours for 200 situations, and the thing that makes citizen queries work at all.
  5. empowers — needs the SRO and Rules corpus, which is not yet scraped.
  6. invalidated_by and commences — need judgments and gazettes. Declared incomplete until then.

One honest limitation, stated up front

Until the judgment and gazette pipelines land, this model can tell a user what a provision says and whether it has been repealed by another statute — but not whether a court has struck it down, and not reliably whether it ever came into force. Those are real gaps with real consequences, and they belong in the interface as a coverage statement rather than in a backlog nobody reads.

Nizam-e-Qanoon Retrieval

The evidence-selection contract between a versioned Pakistani legal corpus and grounded generation: exact when the law is named, hybrid when the issue is described, authority-aware always, and silent when the evidence is not sufficient.

§1Mandate
& boundary

Retrieval decides what the answer is allowed to know

L3 does not answer legal questions. It converts a question into a replayable, legally scoped evidence bundle or a typed abstention. L4 may reason only over that bundle; it may never search around the gate.

Inherited invariantL3 consequence
INV-1Citations are database renderings of stable provision or judgment identifiers. Model-generated citation strings are never accepted.
INV-2Every request exits as GroundingContext or Abstention; no caller can bypass the sufficiency gate.
INV-3Persist query interpretation, filters, corpus version, every candidate rank/score, fusion, rerank, gate version and final evidence IDs.
INV-4Chunks find law; provisions and judgment paragraphs cite law. Re-chunking cannot break a public citation.
INV-5as_of is mandatory, defaults to today, and applies before ranking to versions, territory, authority relations and treatment.

Current corpus boundary. Retrieval chunks and indexes are derived only from v_release_provision_version, joined through v_release_provision and v_release_instrument. As measured on 10 September 2026, that boundary contains 285,156 versions, 300,137 provisions and 3,720 instruments. Active base tables include 2,312 unresolved S7 decisions, identity-linked provenance duplicates and 1,988 canonical contents/body gaps; indexing them would bypass the corpus gate. Reviewed multi-instrument boundaries are materialised and S10 is zero.

Non-goals. L3 does not extract PDFs, publish sources, decide ultimate legal meaning, draft advice, or hide corpus gaps. Those belong to L1/L2, L4, or an explicit human review path.

1.1Ship gates inherited from Document 01

On the locked Pakistani test set: NDCG@10 ≥ 0.75, Recall@50 ≥ 0.90, MRR@10 ≥ 0.70, point-in-time correctness ≥ 0.95, Urdu–English NDCG gap ≤ 0.05, and abstention precision/recall ≥ 0.90 / 0.85. The direct-citation fast path remains below 50 ms; full retrieval fits the existing end-to-end p50/p95 budget of 1.5/4 seconds.

§2Query
contract

Parse deterministically before searching statistically

A legal query is not just text. It is text plus time, forum, territory, source class, cited identifiers and uncertainty. The parser preserves all of it and invents none of it.

type QuerySpec = {
  requestId: UUID; raw: string; normalized: string;
  language: "en" | "ur" | "roman-ur" | "mixed";
  intent: "citation" | "provision" | "issue" | "precedent" | "procedure";
  asOf: LocalDate; knowledgeAsOf: CorpusRevisionId; eventDates: LegalEventDate[];
  jurisdiction?: Jurisdiction; territory?: Territory; legislativeOrigin?: Jurisdiction;
  forum?: CourtId; authorityPerspective?: CourtId; sourceKinds: SourceKind[];
  citations: ParsedCitation[]; terms: TermVariant[];
  unresolved: Ambiguity[]; parserVersion: string;
}

2.1Normalization without evidence destruction

  • Apply Unicode NFC, whitespace and punctuation normalization; preserve the raw query and character map.
  • Normalize Urdu presentation forms and Urdu/Arabic digit variants for matching, never for display.
  • Roman Urdu produces versioned alias candidates from glossary_concept; it is not silently translated into English.
  • Expand known legal abbreviations and spelling variants as parallel terms. Never rewrite an Act, section, court or case number to the “nearest” identifier.
  • Detect explicit dates and phrases such as “in 2020” or “before amendment”; an explicit date overrides the default and is visible to the user.

asOf is the legal-effective date; knowledgeAsOf pins what the system knew for replay. An offence, transaction, accrual and filing may require different event dates, especially for savings and limitation. A date mentioned in narrative is not silently promoted to asOf. Territory, legislative origin, forum and the court from whose perspective authority is assessed are separate fields; guessing one from another is prohibited.

2.2Citation grammar

The grammar recognizes instrument title/number/year, article or section/subsection, rules, case number, neutral citation and reporter reference. A complete parse routes to exact resolution. A partial parse narrows candidates but cannot auto-select among collisions. Reporter references are identifiers only; the system retrieves official judgment text, not copyrighted reporter headnotes.

Ambiguity is data. “Section 9” without an Act, or “LHC 2022” without a case, yields structured choices or AMBIGUOUS_CITATION. Fuzzy matching may suggest; it may not resolve.

§4Candidate
generation

Four independent ways to be found

Each generator receives the same hard-filtered universe and returns ranks, not supposedly comparable scores. Independence is deliberate: exact words, semantic paraphrase, defined terms and legal relationships fail differently.

4.1Lexical

Use ParadeDB BM25 behind ILexicalSearch, with fields for title, heading, provision label, body, citation tokens and case metadata. Phrase matches and exact section labels receive field boosts. Pin the extension version: current ParadeDB SQL is version-sensitive. PostgreSQL ts_rank_cd is a supported fallback, not “equivalent BM25”; flag 32 is rank/(rank+1), not length normalization. Benchmark the fallback independently. Use fixed expression indexes per script/configuration—english for English and simple plus normalized aliases for Urdu/Roman Urdu—because a single English dictionary silently damages recall.

4.2Dense

Embed the original multilingual query directly. HNSW over the inherited bit(1024) representation returns a broad pool; retain the 10× overfetch and rerank with the full-precision query/candidate representation where available. Start hnsw.ef_search at 100, then tune from measured recall. For selective filters, use pgvector iterative scans and an adaptive planner: exact search over a small prefiltered set; HNSW otherwise.

Required L2 correction. ANN predicates hidden behind joins may be applied after index traversal. Materialize a denormalized search_projection carrying legal-unit ID, jurisdiction, validity, publication/status, territory key and embedding on the indexed row. This is a migration owned by Document 03, not an excuse for L3 to post-filter invalid results. Judgment dense retrieval likewise requires a paragraph projection/embedding before the precedent phase can ship.

4.3Definitions and graph

The definition generator resolves English, Urdu and Roman aliases to concepts, then retrieves the definition provision and provisions inside its effective scope. The graph generator starts independently from explicit citation, forum, subject and controlled-term seeds; after fusion, a second deterministic completeness pass expands the winning units. It traverses an allow-list of typed edges—defines, subject_to, notwithstanding, empowers, amends, repeals, cites, followed, distinguished, overruled—for at most two hops. Every hop is time-filtered, direction-aware, and recorded. It expands evidence; it never manufactures relevance from an untyped co-occurrence.

4.4Contents-derived aliases and coverage

The statute’s printed contents list is a high-precision vocabulary source, not answer evidence. Build per-instrument aliases from resolved v_toc rows so a query containing the official marginal heading can recall the linked provision even when OCR or typography weakened its body heading. Exact citation resolution may use the printed label as a consistency check, and context assembly may use neighbouring resolved entries to detect a missing necessary unit. Never index the contents block as substantive body text, never quote it as the governing rule, and never auto-resolve an unmatched v_toc_gap; a gap lowers structural confidence and enters curation. Retrieval traces record whether a candidate came from a body match, a contents-derived alias, or both.

§5Fusion
& reranking

Fuse ranks, then teach relevance in-domain

// generator g ranks legal unit d at rank_g(d), starting at 1
rrf(d) = Σg contains d 1 / (60 + rankg(d))

// collapse multiple chunks without letting long provisions dominate
unitScore = max(chunkRrf) + 0.15 * secondBest(chunkRrf)
candidate = { unitId, bestSpan, generatorRanks, matchedTerms, graphPaths }

RRF is the default because the four score distributions are not calibrated to one another. The constant 60 follows the original formulation and becomes a tested configuration value, not a per-query knob. Keep at most two chunks per legal unit before collapse, cap any one instrument at 40% of the rerank slate, and reserve places for each generator that produced a credible result.

5.1Cross-encoder contract

  • Rerank 50 candidates using a small multilingual cross-encoder; input is query plus heading, hierarchical prefix and the minimal matched span.
  • Return a semantic relevance logit only. Authority, validity and publication are deterministic policy fields, not tokens the model is trusted to interpret.
  • Fine-tune on Pakistani lawyer relevance labels with hard negatives from the same Act, adjacent section, neighbouring jurisdiction, expired version, lower court and later adverse treatment.
  • Version model, tokenizer, prompt template and training-set digest. Calibrate separately for English, Urdu, Roman Urdu and mixed queries where sample size permits.

Cross-encoders are slower but stronger pair scorers, which is why they are a second stage rather than an index. BEIR also cautions against assuming a dense retriever generalizes: BM25 is a strong robust baseline and reranking tends to improve quality at a compute cost. Both findings justify this cascade, not a dense-only design.

§6Legal
correctness

Legal priority is a temporal relation, not a score boost

A semantically perfect passage from the wrong province, date, court or legal version is a wrong result. Those properties constrain eligibility and presentation before relevance is allowed to order anything.

6.1Effective-dated authority matrix

The current Constitution reflects the Twenty-Seventh Amendment (2025). Article 189 now gives decisions of the Federal Constitutional Court on questions/principles of law binding effect over all other courts, including the Supreme Court; Supreme Court decisions bind all other courts except the Federal Constitutional Court. Article 201 governs the binding effect of High Court decisions on subordinate courts, subject to Article 189. Federal Shariat Court decisions have their own constitutional channel. Therefore court enum + numeric rank is not legally adequate.

AuthorityRule {
  decidingCourtId; receivingForumId; subjectMatter;
  validFrom; validTo; effect: "binding" | "persuasive" | "outside-scope";
  constitutionalBasis; sourceVersionId;
}

// resolve as the law of authority stood on the research date
authority = matrix.resolve(decidingCourt, forum, subjectMatter, asOf)
assert authority.sourceVersionId is official and replayable

Architecture correction required. Documents 01 and 03 predate this change and their fixed court hierarchy/enum is stale. Add court, court_alias, court_jurisdiction and effective-dated authority_rule data before precedent retrieval ships. Until then, precedent mode must abstain from claims about binding status.

6.2Commencement, repeal and continuity

Temporal expansion implements the General Clauses Act, 1897 as structured rules, not prompt lore. Its sections 5 and 6 address commencement and the continuing effect of repeal; section 8 carries references through repeal and re-enactment; sections 20A and 21 govern Gazette publication and amendment/rescission of subordinate instruments; sections 22 and 24 cover pre-commencement rules and continuity after repeal/re-enactment. The assembler must fetch the relevant commencement, savings, amendment and delegated-instrument evidence before passing the gate.

6.3Conflict bundles

Do not collapse contrary authorities into one winner using recency or similarity. When eligible decisions conflict, return the leading proposition, contrary decisions, court/bench/date, treatment edges and the authority relation to the user’s forum. The generator must describe the conflict or abstain; it may not synthesize a nonexistent settled rule.

6.4Operative evidence versus historical discovery

Expose two typed modes. operative_evidence may pass the gate only when validity, territory and status permit reliance. historical_discovery may return repealed, superseded, not-yet-commenced or adversely treated material with a prominent status banner, but it cannot ground a statement of current law. Commencement is tri-state: confirmed_in_force, confirmed_not_in_force, or unknown_due_to_coverage. Missing gazette data is never coerced to “not commenced”; an issue requiring that fact abstains.

6.5Boundary with the citator

L3 retrieves official judgment paragraphs and stored treatment edges needed for evidence selection. L5 IPrecedent owns the final treatment classification and “good law” assessment. Until that service and the effective-dated authority data are available, L3 may report recorded treatment facts but must not label a case controlling, overruled or good law on inference alone.

§7Evidence
assembly

Retrieve narrow; assemble complete

Precise spans win retrieval, but legal meaning often depends on a proviso, definition, explanation, schedule or enabling rule outside the winning chunk. Context assembly repairs that boundary deterministically.

type EvidenceItem = {
  legalUnitId; sourceVersionId; sourceUrl; sourceSha256; sourcePages[]; official: true;
  displayCitation; text; matchedOffsets[]; asOf; knowledgeAsOf;
  jurisdiction; territory; authority?: AuthorityFinding;
  treatments[]; inclusionReason; retrievalTraceRef;
}
type GroundingContext = {
  query: QuerySpec; items: EvidenceItem[]; coverage: CoverageFinding;
  conflicts: ConflictSet[]; corpusVersion; retrievalRunId;
}

Choose context by marginal coverage: governing rule first, then a necessary definition/qualification/dependency, then corroborating authority. After reranking about 50 candidates, admit at most about 20 legal units to gate/assembly, subject to token budget and necessary-set coverage. Collapse duplicate quotations and overlapping spans. Order statutes in legal structure, not relevance-score order. A source earns space because it closes a named coverage gap, not because it is the sixth passage saying the same thing.

§8Gate &
abstention

Confidence is calibrated evidence sufficiency

The gate is a small, interpretable classifier over retrieval signals, followed by non-negotiable legal vetoes. Its threshold τ is fitted on a held-out, lawyer-labelled set for the target precision/recall; it is never hand-selected from a few demos and never lowered during degradation.

8.1Features and typed outcomes

Features include top reranker score and margin, generator agreement, exact citation resolution, necessary-set coverage, language/script, query ambiguity, authority resolution, source verification, treatment conflict and corpus coverage. Test calibration error and reliability by intent and language; use group-specific thresholds only with enough held-out examples, otherwise fall back to the conservative global threshold.

type Abstention = {
  code: "AMBIGUOUS_CITATION" | "JURISDICTION_UNRESOLVED" |
        "NO_VALID_VERSION" | "SOURCE_UNVERIFIED" | "FORUM_UNRESOLVED" |
        "ADVERSE_TREATMENT_UNRESOLVED" | "MISSING_DELEGATED_AUTHORITY" |
        "COMMENCEMENT_UNKNOWN" | "CORPUS_GAP" |
        "INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE" | "RETRIEVAL_DEGRADED";
  publicReason; missing: Requirement[]; suggestions: Clarification[];
  confidence; threshold; retrievalRunId;
}

Abstention must be actionable: identify the missing jurisdiction/date/Act/forum or say that the official corpus lacks the material. Never say merely “I cannot answer,” and never fill the gap from model memory.

9.1Adaptive vector execution

// count estimate comes from the denormalized, prefiltered search projection
if estimatedEligible <= EXACT_SCAN_LIMIT:
    exactHammingOrFloatScan(filters, query)       // deterministic recall
else:
    SET LOCAL hnsw.ef_search = tunedEf
    SET LOCAL hnsw.iterative_scan = strict_order
    hnswSearch(filters, query, overfetch = 10 * k)

shadowSample: compare approximate top-k with exact; alert on recall drift

pgvector recommends monitoring approximate recall against exact search. Run exact shadow queries on a small, stratified sample and report recall by jurisdiction/filter selectivity, not only globally. A fast national-average metric can hide a failing Balochistan or historical-date slice.

9.2Degradation

  • Embedder or HNSW unavailable: lexical + definition + graph path, marked lexical_only; normal gate threshold remains.
  • Reranker unavailable: RRF order with a separately calibrated degraded-mode gate; if that calibration has not shipped, abstain.
  • Graph incomplete: provision lookup may continue, but queries requiring definitions, delegated rules, treatment or authority fail the relevant coverage check.
  • Database snapshot changes mid-request: retry the whole retrieval once; never assemble across corpus versions.

9.3Audit and privacy

The retrieval trace stores normalized filters, candidate IDs and stage scores, graph paths, model/index versions, gate features/decision, latency and corpus version. Anonymous mode does not persist raw query text or user ID; use an ephemeral request digest for aggregation. Access logs never contain evidence text. Retention and erasure follow Document 03’s user-data policy while immutable public-corpus identifiers remain replayable.

9.4Offline is a labelled lower tier

The mobile pack uses FTS5 + glossary and may add sqlite-vec with the inherited 384-dimensional device encoder. It has no cross-encoder and is presented as “offline search,” not as quality-equivalent grounded research. Targets remain p95 ≤300 ms for FTS and ≤800 ms for vector search on a 4 GB Android device. Online and offline scores are never mixed or compared as if calibrated.

9.5Exact, versioned caching

A small process-local LRU may cache query embeddings and short-lived retrieval results. The key includes normalized query, language, sorted scopes, exact asOf, source/court filters, corpusVersion, taxonomy, embedding/reranker hashes, retrieval configuration and degradation mode. Do not add Redis for the single-VPS pilot, round dates, share results across full/degraded modes, or cache an inferred jurisdiction as if the user supplied it. Semantic answer caching belongs to Document 05.

10.1Annotation protocol

Two trained legal annotators independently mark the minimal sufficient spans, canonical citable units, required dependencies, temporal/jurisdiction scope and authority. A senior reviewer adjudicates disagreement. Preserve alternate relevant authorities instead of forcing a single-document gold label. LegalBench-RAG’s span-level emphasis is useful, but its contracts domain is not a substitute for this jurisdiction-specific set.

10.2Mandatory ablations

  • BM25 alone, dense alone, BM25+dense, +definitions, +graph, +cross-encoder.
  • Float32 vs halfvec(384) vs bit(1024); binary ships only if reranked NDCG loss is no more than two points and Recall@50 clears target.
  • HNSW vs exact by filter-selectivity bucket; current vs historical dates; each jurisdiction.
  • Direct multilingual retrieval vs any proposed translation hop. Direct remains unless paired evaluation proves otherwise and citation-token fidelity is unchanged.
  • Gate reliability curves and expected calibration error by intent/language, plus selective accuracy as coverage falls.

10.4Feedback without contamination

Lawyer judgments enter a reviewed relevance-label queue, never the live index directly. Split by legal issue/instrument family before training to prevent near-duplicate leakage. Promote a model only on the untouched locked set; run the prior model as baseline and publish deltas by stratum. A click is navigation telemetry, not proof of legal relevance.

11.2Definition of done

  • No path can call generation without a passing bundle; contract test proves the boundary.
  • Every displayed citation resolves to an official stored source and exact citable unit.
  • Replay reconstructs the same candidate ranks from pinned corpus/index/model versions.
  • All quality targets pass overall and by declared jurisdiction/language strata; critical legal-filter errors are zero in the locked set.
  • Load test meets the existing latency/cost budget with cold-cache and degraded-mode runs.
  • Known schema dependencies—search projection, judgment embeddings and authority matrix—are migrated before their modes are enabled.

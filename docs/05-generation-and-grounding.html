Nizam-e-Qanoon Generation and Grounding

A constrained legal explanation engine: the model proposes atomic, evidence-addressed claims; code resolves the law, verifies support, preserves uncertainty, and releases only a complete answer that survived every gate.

Version 1.0Date 30 Aug 2026Layer L4Provides IGenerateRequires IRetrieve · ICorpusRead · ILLMProvider · IInferenceFigures 4
§1Mandate
& invariants

The model writes language; it does not decide truth

L4 accepts a sealed GroundingContext from Document 04 and returns a VerifiedAnswer or a typed GenerationAbstention. It cannot search, broaden scope, repair a failed retrieval gate, infer authority from model memory, or call a domain service.

Inherited ruleExecutable L4 consequence
INV-1The model emits request-local evidence handles only. Citation labels, dates, URLs, reporter references and quoted text are resolved from L2 after generation.
INV-2Abstention never enters L4. Verification failure produces a typed answer failure; there is no general-knowledge fallback.
INV-3Persist context, prompt artifact, provider response, every transformation and verdict so a stored answer is byte-replayable.
INV-4No output contains a chunk ID. Public provenance ends at a provision or judgment paragraph plus source offsets/pages.
INV-5The answer repeats jurisdiction, forum, asOf, knowledge/corpus revision and coverage limits; none may be rewritten by the model.

1.1Release bars

Claim/span support ≥0.97; citation resolution 1.00; material-claim support recall 1.00; no wrong-date, wrong-jurisdiction or invented-source event in the critical set; advice-register violations 0; persisted answers byte-replayable 100%; end-to-end p50/p95 below 1.5/4 seconds; average cost ≤$0.005.

What “replay” means. Hosted generation is stochastic and provider aliases drift. Exact replay means returning and inspecting the stored provider response and verified artifact with all pinned inputs—not asking a model to regenerate identical bytes. Anonymous mode intentionally retains none of those bodies; QA-12 privacy overrides durable replay for that session.

§2Precedents
& boundary

Learn from legal AI without inheriting its claims

The useful precedent is not a vendor slogan. It is a checkable design pattern: stable legal identity, linked primary sources, treatment evidence, atomic attribution, lawyer-built evaluation and visible limits.

System / projectPattern adoptedWhat Nizam-e-Qanoon adds or rejects
Lexis+ / Shepard’sAnswers link into authoritative legal sources and expose citation-treatment signals.Adopt source-first verification; reject any inference that linked citations alone prove the adjacent claim.
Westlaw / CoCounselLegal workflows grounded in a curated research corpus with source links.Adopt inspectability and task profiles; keep Pakistani authority/time/territory as explicit data, not proprietary model behaviour.
CourtListener / RECAPCitation lookup and graph APIs separate source identity from generated prose.Adopt deterministic resolution; add official-source restrictions, bitemporal provisions and Pakistan’s effective-dated authority matrix.
Harvard Caselaw Access ProjectMachine-readable case law and open API/bulk access.Adopt corpus openness as a goal; do not mistake availability for binding effect or current treatment.
EUR-Lex ELIStable human- and machine-readable identifiers, including article-level legal identity.Adopt canonical legal-unit identity; preserve local instrument/provision identifiers and source provenance.
LegalBench / LegalBench-RAGLegal-professional task design and precise source-span retrieval evaluation.Adopt lawyer-led, task-stratified evaluation; build a Pakistan-specific Urdu/English benchmark rather than importing scores.

A preregistered Stanford evaluation found that leading proprietary RAG legal-research tools still produced hallucinations in 17–33% of tested responses. This document therefore follows ADR-6: measured attributed generation, never “hallucination-free.”

2.1Layer boundary

  • L3 decides whether evidence is sufficient and supplies applicable, typed evidence. L4 cannot retrieve.
  • L4 phrases and verifies claims. It may summarize a supplied finding but may not create a legal status.
  • L5 owns precedent treatment/good-law classification, deterministic calculators, clause selection and procedure tables. L5 may call L4; L4 never calls L5.
  • L6 owns sessions, persistence, public API and SSE. It serves exact-citation lookup directly and never sends it through generation.

The current Constitution’s post-2025 authority relations make this boundary essential. The generator receives an AuthorityFinding; it never derives “binding,” “controlling,” “overruled” or “good law” from court names or prose.

§3Typed
contracts

Generate a proposition graph, not a paragraph

Free-form paragraphs mix supported and unsupported clauses under one citation. The model instead produces small atomic claims whose evidence can be checked independently.

type GenerationRequest = {
  requestId; task: "legal_answer" | "provision_explainer" |
                   "judgment_summary" | "draft_phrase";
  context: GroundingContext; contextDigest;
  language: "en" | "ur"; readingLevel: "plain" | "professional";
  register: "legal_information"; riskClass; schemaVersion; anonymous;
}

type AtomicClaim = {
  claimId; text;
  support: { handle: EvidenceHandle; role:
    "primary" | "definition" | "qualification" | "counterauthority" }[];
  epistemic: "source_stated" | "conditional_inference" | "uncertain";
  riskTags: RiskTag[];
}

type DraftBlock = {
  blockId; kind: "short_answer" | "rule" | "conditional_application" |
    "qualification" | "conflict" | "procedure" | "coverage_limit";
  required: boolean; claims: AtomicClaim[];
}

Evidence handles are request-local aliases such as E1, dynamically allow-listed in the JSON Schema. They are not public IDs and the model cannot invent a valid handle. additionalProperties:false, bounded arrays/strings and a pinned JSON Schema 2020-12 dialect make malformed output a typed failure, not an invitation to “repair” prose.

3.1Verified output

type VerifiedAnswer = {
  answerId; requestId; task; language; readingLevel;
  scope: { jurisdiction; territory; forum; asOf; knowledgeAsOf; corpusVersion };
  blocks: VerifiedBlock[]; conflicts: ResolvedConflict[];
  coverageLimits: CoverageLimit[]; escalation?: Escalation;
  disclaimer: DisclaimerId; artifactDigest; pipelineVersions;
}

type VerifiedBlock = {
  blockId; kind; claims: VerifiedClaim[];
  citations: ResolvedCitation[]; verdict: "pass";
}

IGenerate.answer(context, style) remains the public compatibility wrapper. The general internal port becomes generate(GenerationRequest) → VerifiedArtifact | GenerationAbstention so L5 can request a judgment summary or safe phrase without violating the dependency graph. This is a proposed correction to Document 01, not a silent interface change.

§4Trust
pipeline

One-way trust, with code on both sides of the model

4.1Prompt compiler

Compile in this order: immutable policy and output schema; task/scope; evidence manifest; evidence bodies; conflict/coverage findings; user question. User and corpus text are separately delimited and labelled DATA_ONLY. The provider has no tools, search, database access, network action or secret. The prompt asks for concise claims and support handles, never chain-of-thought.

Official does not mean instructional. A court PDF or Gazette can contain text that resembles an instruction, and ingestion errors can expose hidden/bidirectional characters. Retrieved text never gains instruction priority. L1 sanitizes markup; L4 rechecks control characters, isolates bidirectional runs, constrains output and verifies the result.

§5Provider
port

A provider is a replaceable language component

interface ILLMProvider {
  complete(req: ProviderRequest): Promise<ProviderCompletion | ProviderFailure>;
  capabilities(): { schema; snapshot; retention; region; maxContext };
}

ProviderRequest = {
  messages; jsonSchema; schemaVersion; modelSnapshot;
  maxTokens; temperature; deadline; prefixCacheKey;
}
  • Vendor SDK imports exist only in generation/providers/. Each adapter maps the same request and failure taxonomy.
  • Choose a pinned hosted small model for normal explanation and a qualified reasoning tier for complex multi-source scenarios. Selection is by locked-set quality, Urdu parity, latency, retention and price ceiling—not a leaderboard.
  • Use low variance and structured decoding, but never call the output deterministic. JSON shape correctness does not imply semantic correctness.
  • One failover is permitted only to an independently qualified pinned snapshot. No recursive retries, silent “latest” alias, or model that skipped the same safety evaluation.
  • The self-hosted generator is a benchmark/batch/sovereign deployment profile, not the pilot hot path. ADR-2 puts fine-tuning into retrieval/reranking; no generator LoRA is required for MVP.

5.1Admission and privacy

An adapter is enabled only after documenting retention/training terms, data region, incident process, snapshot semantics, rate limits and deletion behaviour. Send no account/profile fields and redact names, CNIC, phone, address or other facts irrelevant to the legal issue. If sensitive traffic has no approved provider, serve current verified artifacts/search or abstain; never downgrade confidentiality.

5.2Budget

Normal output is 4–7 atomic claims, approximately 600 output tokens, with a compact evidence window capped by a configured input budget. Static policy/schema/few-shot prefixes use provider prefix caching. The local NLI batch remains roughly 40 ms for five claims under the inherited estimate. Provider network time dominates, but verification is never skipped to recover the 4-second p95.

§6Citation
& claims

The database, not the model, names the law

A citation is a resolved relationship between a claim and an official, versioned legal unit. It is not decorative text at the end of a paragraph.

// model output
{ claimId: "C2", text: "...", support: [{handle:"E3", role:"qualification"}] }

// request-local handle map; never exposed publicly
E3 -> { legalUnitId, sourceVersionId, allowedOffsets, contextDigest }

// renderer fetches at the pinned revision
ResolvedCitation {
  legalUnitId; sourceVersionId; displayLabel; shortTitle;
  validity; jurisdiction; officialSourceUrl; sourceSha256; sourcePages;
  matchedOffsets; status; authorityFinding?; treatmentCoverage?;
}
  • An unknown, duplicated, out-of-bundle or wrong-version handle rejects the claim and raises defect telemetry.
  • The model cannot output a citation-shaped string, URL, statute date, reporter reference or verbatim quote. If a quote is needed, it requests an allowed span and the renderer slices stored text exactly.
  • Every material atomic claim has at least one supporting relation. A multi-source conclusion cites every source necessary for the conjunction.
  • Support and relevance are separate: each cited item must actually contribute. Extra citations cannot launder an unsupported proposition.

6.1Answer order

Permanent scope and corpus coverage; concise conditional answer; governing rule; required definitions/provisos/exceptions/delegated instruments; conditional application to user-stated facts; conflict/unknowns; neutral next steps and referral. Deadlines, custody/liberty risks and non-operative status surface before background. User allegations remain “facts as stated,” never findings.

No chain-of-thought record. The proposition/evidence graph is the auditable reasoning surface. Hidden model reasoning is neither legal authority nor reliable provenance and is not requested, stored or shown.

§7Verification
cascade

Atomic verification, then an answer-level veto

TierChecksFailure
T0JSON Schema, size/Unicode bounds, allowed block kinds and evidence handles; no URLs/HTML/citation-shaped strings.Whole completion rejected as SCHEMA_INVALID; no repair prompt
T1Exact quote offsets; numbers, dates, durations and amounts; identity/version/as-of; jurisdiction; negation; shall/may; exception/proviso; required evidence roles.Claim rejected with deterministic reason
T2Local multilingual NLI over the atomic claim and its cited premises; contradiction/neutral/low score fails. Each citation also tested for necessity.Claim rejected; NLI is a filter, never proof
T3Independent adversarial verifier tries to falsify liability, limitation/deadline, eligibility, custody, authority/conflict, eviction, family-status and irreversible-transfer claims.unsupported or ambiguous rejects the claim
T4Register, policy, whole-answer coverage/coherence, conflict and escalation checks.Drop optional context or fail complete answer

A missing optional background claim may be dropped. Failure of the direct answer, governing rule, required qualification/exception, conflict disclosure or procedural precondition produces ANSWER_INCOMPLETE_AFTER_VERIFICATION. “Partially supported legal proposition” is never a shippable verdict; only an explicit coverage-limit block may describe uncertainty.

§8Legal
safety

Preserve legal force, not just meaning

Generator mayGenerator may not
Faithfully paraphrase a supplied operative ruleInfer commencement, repeal, territorial reach or invalidation
State a conditional: “If X is established, the provision states Y”Find facts, decide credibility, predict an outcome or guarantee relief
Combine compatible rules with all necessary supportChoose a winner among an unresolved ConflictSet
Explain typed authority/treatment findings from L5Call a case binding, controlling, overruled or good law from prose alone
Describe a source-backed procedure neutrallyCalculate fees, limitation, shares, forum or clauses assigned to deterministic L5 services
Identify missing facts and official coverage gapsTurn corpus silence into “no law exists” or fill a gap from model memory

8.1Information, not representation

The Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Act, 1973 reserves practice of law to advocates. This product therefore adopts a conservative information boundary, without pretending that the statute alone conclusively classifies every automated explanation. Launch requires Pakistani counsel review. The output register is “the law provides” and “the source states,” not personalized commands, tactics, document-signing approval or advocate impersonation.

A structural, non-dismissible disclaimer states jurisdiction/date, corpus coverage, that facts were not independently verified, and that the output is legal information rather than representation. Wording in English and Urdu is approved and versioned by counsel. It is never model-authored and never treated as a substitute for correct output.

8.2Escalation

Escalate for arrest/liberty/bail, an imminent filing/appeal/limitation date, violence or immediate safety, eviction, irreversible waiver/transfer, child/family status, constitutional validity, unresolved forum/jurisdiction, contrary authority, incomplete delegated law/commencement/treatment coverage, or a request for representation/tactics. Return why human review matters, what material is present/missing, useful dates/documents to gather, and a curated official legal-aid/advocate route. Contact links are maintained data, never generated.

8.3Bilingual equivalence

English and native Urdu are two renderings of the same proposition IDs, evidence relations, modality and caveats. Enacted English text remains verbatim and visibly distinct; Urdu is labelled explanation unless an authentic Urdu source exists. XNLI Urdu is only a generic checkpoint smoke test. Shipping requires lawyer-labelled Pakistani legal claim/span parity, including shall/may, negation, provisos, dates and mixed-script citations. Roman Urdu is accepted at L3; L4 returns native Urdu rather than unstable Roman legal prose.

§9Cache &
offline tier

Reuse proof, not merely similar prose

9.1Three different caches

  • Provider prefix cache: a billing/latency optimization for static policy, schema and examples; no correctness semantics.
  • Exact verified-artifact cache: reuse only when query intent/scope, exact dates, evidence/context fingerprint, conflict/coverage findings, language/register and every pipeline version match.
  • Curated answer tier: batch-generated, fully verified artifacts admitted to the signed offline pack; current dependency checks and release review required.

A semantic nearest match may nominate an artifact, but it never authorizes reuse. Require semantic-equivalence confirmation, exact legal-scope equality, live dependency fingerprint and locked-set cache precision ≥0.95. Disable semantic reuse for precedent/conflict, limitation, liberty/custody and other high-risk scenarios until separately proven safe. Show the canonical matched question and corpus/build date.

9.2Required L2 correction

The current stored_answer.cited_provisions[] and one global verifier_score cannot express judgment/schedule dependencies or per-claim proof. Normalize answer_dependency, answer_claim, answer_claim_evidence, verification_result and artifact_invalidation. Publication invalidates affected artifacts in the same transaction. The offline tier admits only all-pass blocks; online uncertainty appears only as explicit coverage limits.

Conceptually this forms a W3C PROV-style graph: evidence/prompt/answer are entities; retrieve/generate/verify/render are activities; providers/models/curators are agents; each artifact is explicitly derived from its inputs. MVP keeps relational tables rather than introducing RDF.

10.1Anonymous mode

No user ID, query, prompt/provider body, answer body, cache insertion, analytics content or durable generation row. Keep only ephemeral in-memory state and content-free operational counters, then destroy them. OpenTelemetry spans never contain query/evidence/answer text; GenAI semantic conventions are still evolving, so the project pins its own stable, content-free attributes.

10.2Report and incident loop

A report carries answerId/requestId into a review queue with the exact artifact, claim/evidence graph and verifier trace. A reviewer can invalidate an artifact immediately, classify root cause—corpus, retrieval, authority, generation, verification or UI—and create a regression fixture. User feedback never directly updates prompts, labels or the live answer tier.

§12Evaluation
& red team

Measure support, completeness and usefulness separately

A system that answers nothing has perfect factual precision. Report risk against useful-answer coverage, and isolate L4 from retrieval so a generation defect cannot hide inside an end-to-end average.

12.1Two benchmark tracks

  • Oracle-context: lawyers provide the complete evidence bundle. Measures generation, attribution, legal modality, verification and register.
  • End-to-end: real L3 output. Measures the full ceiling, including corpus/retrieval/coverage failure.

Stratify both by English/Urdu/mixed output, Roman Urdu input, task, statute/precedent/procedure, present/historical law, jurisdiction, conflict, answerable/unanswerable and risk class. Split by instrument/case family and legal issue to prevent near-duplicate leakage. Two Pakistani legal annotators independently mark atomic support, minimal spans, citation necessity/completeness, material qualifications, applicability and advice register; a senior reviewer adjudicates and agreement is reported.

12.3Ablations

Free prose vs constrained schema; block vs atomic claims; T1 only vs +T2 vs +T3; same vs independent adversarial verifier; raw vs data-only/no-tools context; exact vs fuzzy cache; provider/model snapshots; English vs Urdu; oracle vs retrieved context. Report paired deltas with confidence intervals. A prompt/model/verifier change cannot merge if any critical stratum regresses.

13.1Definition of done

  • No code path generates after L3 abstention or for exact-citation lookup.
  • No public citation, quote, date or URL originates in model output.
  • Every material claim is atomic, supported, resolved and verified; required-claim failure aborts the answer.
  • Whole-answer checks preserve qualifications, conflict, temporal/jurisdiction scope and deterministic-service boundaries.
  • Only verified artifacts/blocks are released; raw provider tokens never reach the client.
  • Persisted artifacts replay exactly; anonymous mode leaves no content behind.
  • Dependency invalidation covers provisions, judgments, schedules, authority/treatment, corpus and pipeline versions.
  • Locked lawyer-labelled tests, red team, latency, cost and bilingual gates pass in CI.

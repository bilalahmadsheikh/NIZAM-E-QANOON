§1Mandate
& proof

Translate law into traceable decisions, never invisible opinion

L5 converts facts already supplied by a user into narrow domain results: treatment evidence, a calculation, an ordered procedure, or a document assembled from approved clauses. It does not retrieve open-ended law, invent facts, decide a dispute, predict a judge, or replace an advocate.

InvariantExecutable consequenceProof basis
D-INV-1Every conclusion carries jurisdiction, asOf, rule-pack version, corpus version, source provisions and a human-readable trace.Pakistan Code directs doubtful users to the relevant Gazette; the Publication of Laws of Pakistan Act creates the Laws of Pakistan Cell.
D-INV-2Rules and tables are effective-dated and territorial. A federal consolidation cannot silently supply a provincial fee or stamp rate.Court-Fees Act ss.20, 26, 35 and provincial amendments; provincial Stamp Act schedules; KP fee abolition is a concrete divergence.
D-INV-3A precedent status is an evidence-bearing signal with coverage, not a green/red badge.Constitution arts.189 and 201 establish binding relations; later treatment, bench composition, question of law and corpus gaps remain separate facts.
D-INV-4The model may phrase or summarize; it cannot choose a clause, deadline, rate, forum, authority class or treatment.NJPMC’s 2026 Guidelines position AI as assistive while preserving human judgment, accountability and transparency.
D-INV-5No result labelled “reviewed,” “file-ready” or “good law” without the corresponding human workflow and evidence.Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Act 1973 s.22; product boundary requires counsel review rather than an overbroad claim about all automation.

Legal status of the executable rule. The Gazette or judgment remains authoritative. A RuleVersion is Nizam-e-Qanoon’s reviewed interpretation of that source, not a new source of law. Every screen and artifact must preserve that distinction.

1.1Release bars

Source resolution 1.00; published rules with two-person legal approval 1.00; arithmetic and calendar fixtures 1.00; wrong province/date/court hierarchy in the critical set 0; unexplained precedent edge 0; clause without rule and source 0; stale result after dependency publication 0; English–Urdu material-parity gap ≤0.03.

§2Pakistani
source order

Authority begins with the promulgated source, not the convenient website

A domain result is only as defensible as its source resolution. Pakistan Code is an essential official access layer, but its own disclaimer says material is under review and doubtful questions should return to Gazette notifications. The service therefore stores source rank and never collapses an access copy into promulgation proof.

RankSource classUsePublication rule

P1Official consolidated codeCurrent readable text and amendment footnotes from Pakistan Code, Punjab Laws, Sindh Code and KP CodeUseful access copy; compare to P0 before a calculator or clause rule becomes active
P2Official administrative portalCurrent operational rates, forms, e-stamp workflow, court rules, calendars and filing instructionsMay drive operations only when its issuing authority and effective date are captured

2.1Effective authority matrix after the Twenty-Seventh Amendment

Deciding courtBinding reach encoded by L5Guard
Federal Constitutional CourtUnder current Article 189(1), a decision on a question/principle of law binds all other courts, including the Supreme Court.Only matters within the actual decision; capture majority, separate opinions and issue proposition.
Supreme CourtUnder Article 189(2), a decision on a question/principle of law binds all other courts except the Federal Constitutional Court.Do not apply the pre-November-2025 hierarchy to an asOf date after the amendment.
High CourtArticle 201 binds courts subordinate to that High Court to the stated extent.Territorial High Court and subordinate forum must match; other High Courts are persuasive unless another rule applies.
Federal Shariat Court / specialist hierarchyApply the Constitution and enabling statute’s typed appellate and binding relations.No generic numeric “court rank”; domain and appeal route are required.

The hierarchy is bitemporal. A case decided before the amendment keeps its decision date; the question whether and how it controls a later matter is evaluated against the constitutional and transitional framework applicable to the query date. L5 consumes explicit court-domain edges from Document 03b and does not infer them from a court-name string.

§4Executable
law model

A rule is a proposition tied to text, scope and time

Do not start with a general-purpose expert-system language. Start with a small, typed expression grammar sufficient for comparison, dates, money, set membership and explicit priorities. Preserve an export mapping to LegalRuleML concepts, but keep the runtime understandable to Pakistani lawyers and application engineers.

type RuleVersion = {
  ruleId; version; domain; status: "draft" | "review" | "published" | "retired";
  jurisdiction; territory[]; forumKinds[]; matterKinds[];
  validFrom; validTo?; transactionFrom; transactionTo?;
  authority: AuthorityAnchor[];        // provision/judgment paragraph/Gazette page
  proposition;                         // lawyer-readable controlled English
  when: Expr; unless?: Expr[]; priority: PrioritySpec;
  effect: Effect; requiredInputs: FieldSpec[];
  interpretationNotes[]; testFixtureIds[];
  draftedBy; reviewedBy[]; approvedAt?; digest;
}

type Effect =
  | { kind: "period"; value: Duration; accrual: AccrualRule }
  | { kind: "money"; formula: MoneyExpr; rounding: RoundingRule }
  | { kind: "forum"; courtTypeId; filingPlaceRule }
  | { kind: "require_clause" | "forbid_clause"; clauseId }
  | { kind: "procedure_step"; stepId; prerequisites[] }
  | { kind: "authority_relation"; relation; target };

type DecisionTrace = {
  inputDigest; scope; candidates[]; applicable[]; rejected[];
  evaluations: { ruleVersion; condition; inputs; result }[];
  conflicts[]; assumptions[]; unknowns[]; result; dependencyDigest;
}

4.1No arbitrary code in published content

  • Expressions are parsed to an allow-listed AST. No eval, imports, reflection, SQL, network, filesystem or model call.
  • Money uses decimal arithmetic with an explicit currency and rounding rule. Dates use civil-date types, not local timestamps.
  • Every input is typed, unit-labelled and either user-asserted, corpus-derived or administrative data. The trace distinguishes them.
  • Priority is explicit: constitutional hierarchy, special/local law, non-obstante/subject-to edges, later effective provision, or unresolved conflict. A row order is never legal priority.
  • Unknown facts propagate. They do not become false. The result is complete, conditional, not_covered or conflict.

Research precedent. OASIS LegalRuleML explicitly models jurisdiction, temporality, defeasibility, negation, authority and the link between a legal rule and its natural-language source. OECD Rules as Code and New Zealand Better Rules likewise insist that machine-consumable rules remain alongside human-readable rules. We adopt those properties, not their full syntax.

§5Precedent
citator

Answer “what happened to this proposition?”, not “is this case good?”

A judgment may remain valid on one proposition and be doubted, distinguished or displaced on another. The citator’s unit is therefore a normalized legal proposition linked to exact paragraphs, not the whole case.

type TreatmentEdge = {
  citingOpinionId; citingParagraphIds[];
  citedOpinionId; citedParagraphIds[]; propositionId;
  treatment: "followed" | "applied" | "approved" | "affirmed" |
             "distinguished" | "doubted" | "disapproved" |
             "overruled" | "reversed" | "explained" | "cited";
  polarity: "positive" | "negative" | "neutral";
  effectScope: "proposition" | "holding" | "order" | "unknown";
  extraction: { method; confidence; modelVersion? };
  reviewStatus: "machine_candidate" | "lawyer_verified" | "contested";
  evidenceQuoteOffsets[]; sourceVersionId;
}

5.1Signal contract

type AuthoritySignal = {
  judgmentId; propositionSignals[];
  summary: "no_adverse_treatment_found" | "adverse_treatment_found" |
           "mixed_or_distinguished" | "coverage_insufficient";
  supportingEdges[]; adverseEdges[]; unresolvedEdges[];
  hierarchyApplied[]; coverage: { courts[]; from; to; documents; missingSources[] };
  generatedAt; asOf; corpusVersion; traceId;
}

no_adverse_treatment_found means exactly that within declared coverage. It never becomes “still good law.” Machine extraction can prioritize review, but overruled, reversed and proposition-level negative treatment require lawyer verification before public display.

5.2Ingestion and identity

Use official judgment portals for the Federal Constitutional Court, Supreme Court and each High Court. Preserve case number, decision date, upload date, bench, author, separate/dissenting opinions, disposition and source hash. Reporter citations are aliases. Paragraph anchors are canonical where the judgment numbers paragraphs; otherwise create stable source offsets while retaining page coordinates.

§6Judgment
profiles

Summaries are verified profiles, not editorial headnotes

L5 chooses the schema and supplies the judgment; L4 may phrase fields, but Document 05’s atomic support and whole-answer veto apply. No summary field can create a treatment or authority edge.

FieldEvidence ruleFailure behavior
Procedural postureCase header, impugned order and disposition paragraphsUnknown if source pages are missing
Material factsEach fact linked to a paragraph; party allegation labelled as allegationDrop unsupported fact
Questions/issuesExpress issue paragraph or clearly qualified reconstructionMark inferred_issue, never quotation
ArgumentsKeep each party and court treatment separateNo laundering an argument into a holding
Reasoning and holdingMajority paragraphs and exact proposition supportWhole profile fails if decisive qualification is unsupported
OrderOperative final paragraphs onlyNo inference from case metadata

Profiles are regenerated only when the judgment source, schema, verifier or correction record changes. A later judgment changes the citator’s treatment view, not the historical text of the earlier judgment profile.

§7Limitation
engine

A deadline is an event calculation with legal branches

The Limitation Act’s First Schedule supplies periods and starting points, but a useful calculator must also model the mandatory bar in section 3, court closure in section 4, the limited extension route in section 5, disability, exclusions, fraud, acknowledgment, payment, continuing wrongs and the special/local-law rule in section 29.

StageDeterministic workRequired evidence / guard
LIM-0Resolve claim/application/appeal type, relief, forum, governing special law and asOf.Ambiguous cause maps to candidates; service never chooses a cause of action for the user.
LIM-1Select applicable Schedule article or special/local period and accrual rule.Section 29(2) determines which general provisions remain available when a special/local law differs.
LIM-2Construct event timeline: accrual, knowledge, death/representation, disability, prior proceeding, stay, acknowledgment and payment.Every date labelled asserted, documented or inferred; inferred dates cannot yield a final deadline.
LIM-3Apply included/excluded day rules, calendar arithmetic and effective court closures.Official court calendar version and timezone; no timestamp arithmetic.
LIM-4Evaluate exclusions/restarts in legal order and retain every rejected branch.Section 5 is not a general power to extend every suit; service states the relevant proceeding class.
LIM-5Return base date, adjustments, computed last day, safer filing-by date and uncertainty.Where a judicial finding such as sufficient cause, good faith, fraud or due diligence is required, return conditional branches and advocate escalation.

7.1Result shape

type LimitationResult = {
  status: "complete" | "conditional" | "not_covered" | "conflict";
  governingRule; period; accrualEvent; baseDeadline?;
  adjustments: { kind; from; to; days; authority; condition? }[];
  finalDeadline?; conservativeFileBy?; calendarVersion;
  assumptions[]; disputedInputs[]; alternatives[]; authorities[]; traceId;
}

Never market a limitation date as guaranteed. Section 3 makes lateness consequential even when limitation is not pleaded, while sections 4–25 and special/local laws can alter computation. The product must show the full calculation and tell users to file earlier and obtain legal review.

§8Fees, stamp
& forum

Province, relief and date are mandatory calculator inputs

The federal-era Court-Fees and Stamp Acts are only the trunk. Provincial finance acts, amendment acts, rules, remissions and operational valuation systems alter the result. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s Court Fees (Abolition) Ordinance is proof that a national rate table would be structurally wrong.

Stamp dutyProvince/ICT, instrument classification, consideration/value, property location/use, execution date, partiesStamp Act and current provincial Schedule I; Gazette notifications; official e-stamp parametersShow classification and rate; link to official payment/verification portal rather than issuing a stamp
RegistrationInstrument, property, term/value, location, execution/presentation datesRegistration Act ss.17–35 plus provincial rules and fee notificationsReturn compulsory/optional/unknown and presentation requirements; not title assurance
ForumMatter, statute, relief, value, territory, parties, subject property and dateConstitution/enabling statute; CPC ss.15–20; civil-court laws; special tribunals and local limitsReturn candidate forum and unresolved venue facts; no prediction that jurisdiction objection will succeed

Punjab’s official PLRA e-stamping service already calculates stamp duty, CVT and related charges and issues a verifiable challan/e-stamp. Nizam-e-Qanoon should deep-link or reconcile against that official service where available, not compete with it as a payment authority. Differences between our estimate and an official portal are a blocking incident for the affected rule pack.

§9Document

The interview gathers facts; reviewed rules construct the instrument

The document factory follows the proven guided-interview pattern used by docassemble, but adds Pakistan-specific source, province, language, stamp, registration and provenance controls. AI can simplify a question; it never alters the field schema or operative clause set.

9.2Rendering and safety

  • DOCX is the editable professional artifact; PDF is a preview/finalized rendering. XeLaTeX or an equivalently tested engine handles Urdu, mixed direction and embedded fonts.
  • Rendering runs without network, shell escape, user-controlled template paths or executable macros. Uploaded images are decoded and re-encoded.
  • Artifact provenance lists every clause ID/version, rule that fired, source anchors, omitted optional clauses, duty estimate, template digest and whether an advocate reviewed it.
  • A material field change invalidates prior review and render signatures. Anonymous sessions retain no answers or artifacts after delivery.

§11Rule
publication

Treat legal content changes like signed production releases

A Git commit is not legal approval, and a lawyer’s email is not a reproducible deployment. Rule publishing combines source evidence, structured review, executable tests, maker-checker separation and atomic activation.

11.1Corrections are append-only

Never overwrite a published rule. Publish a successor with transaction time, legal validity interval and reason; retain the old digest for replay. An emergency quarantine can stop serving a rule immediately without pretending its historical publication never happened.

11.2Challenge workflow

Every result exposes “challenge this rule/treatment.” The challenge carries only the trace and user-selected issue unless the user elects to add facts. A lawyer can mark confirmed, needs_source, classification_error, outdated or interpretive_dispute. Interpretive disputes stay visible and do not become silent edits.

13.1Runtime and security

  • Normal calculators and published guides run in-process against immutable pack snapshots; no network call and p95 under 150 ms.
  • Precedent graph queries run in PostgreSQL with bounded depth, court/date filters and statement timeouts; summaries are asynchronous or cached verified artifacts.
  • Document compilation runs in an isolated worker with CPU/memory/time limits, read-only templates, no network, no shell escape and content-addressed outputs.
  • A request pins one rule-pack manifest and corpus version. Mid-request publication cannot create a mixed result.
  • Only L6 authorizes sessions and persistence. L5 accepts an anonymous policy flag and returns artifacts without creating a hidden domain-side account or history.

13.2Offline

Signed offline packs may include published procedure guides, limitation/rate tables, court competence and pre-approved document interview schemas. They exclude the server-scale precedent graph and novel document rendering. Each offline result shows pack version and knowledge date; revoked/quarantined rule IDs are carried by signed patches.

For each launch domain, two Pakistani advocates independently annotate source, proposition, scope, exceptions and expected result; a senior reviewer adjudicates. Report agreement and unresolved interpretive disputes. Test data separates rules used to author implementation from a locked challenge set. Production challenges feed triage, never automatic learning.

14.3Definition of done

  • Every result identifies controlling sources, territory, date, rules, inputs, assumptions, unknowns and coverage.
  • No executable rule is active from an unverified consolidation alone.
  • No model chooses authority, treatment, clause, deadline, fee, stamp rate, forum or procedure step.
  • No “good law” or “no precedent” claim replaces evidence and declared coverage.
  • No document clause lacks a published rule and exact legal source.
  • Rule changes publish atomically, invalidate every dependent result and preserve historical replay.
  • Critical temporal, territorial, mutation, bilingual and sandbox suites pass with independent legal review.

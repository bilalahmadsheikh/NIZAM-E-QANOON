§0Mandate
& proof

The API coordinates legal truth; it never manufactures it

L6 is the sole synchronous entry point and the sole authority for identity, persistence and release. It converts stable layer contracts into a client-safe protocol while preserving every abstention, qualification, source version and inability returned below it.

Normative language

Must and shall are release requirements. An outcome is a valid legal result such as abstention or insufficient facts; a problem is a protocol or system fault. L6 must never translate the first into the second merely to simplify a client.

§1Boundary
& modules

A modular monolith with ports, not internal HTTP

One FastAPI deployment owns routes and orchestration; the legal modules remain ordinary typed Python dependencies. Inference and rendering are separate workers because they have different resource and isolation needs, not because service boundaries are fashionable.

§4Identity
& sessions

Phone-first is an access choice, not a claim of strong identity

SMS proves temporary control of a number; it does not prove the subscriber is the named person or an enrolled advocate. The backend keeps account login, professional verification and privileged-administrator assurance as separate facts.

ControlCitizen / advocate accountPrivileged staff
Initial sign-inE.164 Pakistani number; single-use SMS OTP; email optional.Invite only; verified organizational identity.
AssurancePossession-level account. Advocate status is a separately reviewed credential with issuer and expiry.Phishing-resistant passkey/WebAuthn preferred; TOTP recovery; SMS alone forbidden.
Access tokenFive-minute signed token containing opaque subject, session id, assurance, scopes and token version; no phone, email or professional data.
RefreshOpaque 256-bit rotating token, hashed at rest, one family per device. Reuse revokes the whole family and creates a security event.
OTPChallenge-specific salted hash, ten-minute maximum, accepted once, five verification attempts, resend cooldown and progressive account/IP/device controls.
RecoveryNew-number recovery is delayed and risk-reviewed; saved legal data is not disclosed through an OTP-only shortcut.Two-person recovery with recorded evidence.

4.1Anonymous execution is a different composition root

Anonymous entry creates an opaque, SameSite-bound session and constructs AnonymousExecutionPolicy. That graph binds ephemeral in-memory buffers and no-op content telemetry; it does not bind answer history, drafts, user sync, semantic-cache insertion, billing identity or durable audit bodies. Joining later starts a fresh account context; it never imports anonymous history without an explicit, itemized user action.

Privacy beats replay

For anonymous work, retain content-free counters only: route class, coarse result class, latency bucket and service health. No query digest intended for later correlation, no evidence text, no answer body and no device fingerprint. An ephemeral request identifier may exist in memory only for the lifetime of the response.

§5Authorization
& tenancy

Every object lookup is an authorization decision

Roles express capability; ownership, matter membership, jurisdiction and workflow state decide the object. Route decorators alone are insufficient, so policy checks occur in the application service and PostgreSQL RLS supplies a second boundary for user data.

Opaque identifiers are not authorization. Every GET /drafts/{id}, event stream, artifact URL and sync record verifies actor-object relation. Signed object-store URLs are short-lived, single-purpose and minted only after authorization; storage buckets are never public.

§6Answer
orchestration

Persist the proof before releasing the legal prose

The backend streams progress, not speculative model tokens. Retrieval may abstain; generation may abstain; verification may drop a claim or veto the whole artifact. Only a complete verified artifact crosses the release barrier.

6.1Two-step streaming contract

POST /v1/answers validates, idempotently creates the operation and returns 202 with its resource and event URL. GET /v1/answers/{id}/events serves text/event-stream. This corrects the conceptual one-request sequence in Document 01 so standard EventSource reconnection and authorization can be implemented cleanly.

event: answer.status
id: 3
data: {"request_id":"...","phase":"verifying","progress":"claims"}

event: answer.block
id: 4
data: {"block":{...verified block with resolved citations...}}

event: answer.completed
id: 5
data: {"answer_id":"...","artifact_digest":"...","scope":{...}}
  • Allowed events: answer.status, answer.abstained, answer.block, answer.completed, answer.cancelled and answer.problem. Raw provider deltas are impossible at this boundary.
  • Events have monotonically increasing per-answer ids. Last-Event-ID replays only after actor authorization and only from the stored verified journal.
  • Anonymous replay exists only within the same ephemeral session and process for five minutes. Loss returns 410 anonymous_stream_expired; the user may restart, not recover hidden persistence.
  • Heartbeat comments every 15 seconds carry no content. Proxy buffering is disabled. Disconnect cancels provider work when no durable consumer remains, except after the release barrier where completion is cheap and deterministic.

§9Audit
& challenge

A replay is a proof graph, not another model call

Hosted models are stochastic and aliases drift. Exact replay means presenting the stored provider response and verified artifact with the same pinned inputs, transformations and decisions—never asking a model to reproduce the text.

Audit elementMinimum fieldsIntegrity
AdmissionRequest id, actor class, legal scope, corpus/rule snapshots, client and API versions.Canonical JSON digest.
RetrievalParser, filters, candidates and all stage scores, graph paths, gate features/version/decision.Immutable trace reference.
GenerationContext digest, prompt artifact, provider/model snapshot, parameters, raw structured response, cost/timing.Encrypted body + digest.
VerificationClaim/evidence graph, deterministic and NLI checks, thresholds, verdicts, dropped claims and completeness result.Append-only verdict rows.

A reviewer can immediately mark an artifact withdrawn, which blocks new reads and invalidates dependent cached/offline answers. Corrected artifacts are new versions; history is never overwritten. User feedback never edits prompts, labels, rules or the live corpus automatically.

Account deletion starts an auditable erasure workflow covering primary user tables, object artifacts, search/cache entries and encryption keys. These are product controls pending a separately approved retention schedule—not invented statutory periods.

13.1Idempotency

The key is bound to actor scope, route, API major and canonical request digest. Same key and digest returns the stored status/body; same key with another digest returns 409. In-flight duplicates wait briefly or receive 409 with the operation URL. Records expire after 24 hours for authenticated commands; anonymous keys live only in the ephemeral session.

13.2Caching

  • Exact legal cache keys include normalized input, explicit and inferred scope separately, exact as_of, corpus/rule/taxonomy versions, pipeline/model hashes, language, reading level, entitlements and degradation mode.
  • No shared cache for private query text, matters, drafts, user sync or review cases. Public immutable law may use CDN public, immutable; authenticated data uses private, no-store unless explicitly safe.
  • Semantic similarity may nominate a verified answer but cannot authorize reuse; L4’s equivalence, scope and dependency gates remain mandatory.
  • No Redis at pilot. Process-local LRU handles small public metadata; PostgreSQL owns shared idempotency, quotas, jobs and cache records. Add a network cache only after measured lock/contention evidence.

Metering is an append-only usage ledger separate from payment: actor/entitlement, operation class, reserved/actual units, provider cost and reversal. B2B API keys and charging stay disabled in v1, but scopes and units exist so institutional exposure later does not require rewriting authorization.

Secrets arrive at runtime, never in source, images, Compose files or telemetry. Separate credentials exist for API, migrator, corpus publisher, workers and backup. The API has read-only corpus grants; the renderer has no database or network credential; the pack signer is absent from the API container.

Evidence preservation

Security response records hashes, UTC timestamps, actor/session identifiers, immutable public-resource ids and access events. It does not create an unrestricted copy of every user’s legal content. Incident evidence is separately authorized, encrypted, scoped and expired.

§16Runtime
& config

Stateless API processes; state and truth remain explicit

The production shape inherits Documents 09a and 09b: Python 3.12 slim image locked with uv, four Uvicorn workers on the pilot host, PostgreSQL as the only operational state store and R2/MinIO for content-addressed blobs.

  • Async routes perform I/O through async clients. CPU-bound embedding, reranking, verification and rendering never run in the API event loop.
  • Startup runs no migrations. A separate migrator role applies reviewed Alembic migrations before readiness. All schema objects and roles originate in migrations.
  • Configuration is typed, validated and printed only as a redacted fingerprint. Environment differences select adapters and enabled corpus scopes, never alternate business logic.
  • Graceful shutdown stops admission, drains short requests, marks streams reconnectable, returns unleased jobs and respects a bounded termination window.
  • Database pools have per-worker ceilings; transactions are short; long legal work pins version ids rather than holding snapshots open.

17.1Definition of done

  • Every operation in §7 exists in OpenAPI or is explicitly phase-gated; no undocumented route ships.
  • All five master invariants and QA-7, QA-8, QA-9, QA-10, QA-11, QA-12 and QA-13 have executable backend evidence.

  • Generated clients pass contract diff, the legal locked set has no critical regression, and rollback is rehearsed before pilot traffic.

Primary sources and implementation standards

Legal sources establish the risk posture; technical standards establish interoperable mechanisms. Product safeguards that go beyond current enacted Pakistani law are identified as policy choices, not attributed to a statute.

  1. Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan — Article 14 on dignity and privacy of the home; current consolidated text. National Assembly official PDF.
  2. Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 — current Pakistan Code access copy incorporating the Prevention of Electronic Crimes (Amendment) Act, 2025. Ministry of Law and Justice PDF.
  3. Electronic Transactions Ordinance, 2002 — electronic documents, communications, signatures, integrity and certification framework. Pakistan Code official PDF.
  4. Qanun-e-Shahadat Order, 1984 — Articles 73 and 164 and electronic-system/modern-device evidence. Pakistan Code official PDF.
  5. Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Act, 1973 — professional-practice framework. Pakistan Code official PDF.
  6. Pakistan Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Rules, 1976 — professional conduct, including rule 146 concerning confidential information obtained from clients/former clients. Pakistan Bar Council official PDF and official catalogue.
  7. Personal Data Protection Bill — the Ministry of IT legislation register lists the May 2023 instrument as a draft; this document therefore does not treat it as enacted law. Official status register.
  8. National Guidelines for Use of Artificial Intelligence in Judicial Institutions of Pakistan — human-centric, assistive, transparent, accountable and privacy-protecting principles. Law and Justice Commission of Pakistan; adoption in the NJPMC official decision record.
  9. OpenAPI Specification 3.2.0 — language-neutral HTTP interface description and current specification used for generated clients and contract diffing. OpenAPI Initiative specification.
  10. RFC 9457 — Problem Details for HTTP APIs and application/problem+json. RFC Editor.

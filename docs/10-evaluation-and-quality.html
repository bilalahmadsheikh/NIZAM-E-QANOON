§0Mandate
& authority

Quality is permission to release, not a score after release

Document 10 is independent of the implementation layers. It does not improve a result, select a model or repair a corpus. It measures a versioned candidate against declared legal and engineering risks, then returns pass, limited_scope or reject with reproducible evidence.

Standing ruleEvaluation consequenceNon-negotiable evidence
INV-1Generated citation strings never receive partial credit. Identity and quotation are resolved against L2 bytes.Every displayed citation resolves; every quote matches the stored span exactly.
INV-2An answer emitted after a failed sufficiency gate is a critical architecture violation, even if its prose happens to be correct.Control-flow and mutation tests prove the abstention branch cannot call generation.
INV-3Evaluation replays stored authenticated artifacts; it never asks a stochastic provider to recreate old bytes.Request, scope, candidates, evidence, prompt, provider response, verdicts and artifact digests remain linked.
INV-4Golden labels end at provisions, judgment paragraphs and source spans, not volatile chunks.A re-chunk migration invalidates zero labels, citations, bookmarks or answer dependencies.
INV-5Correct law from the wrong date, territory, forum or authority perspective is wrong.Every fixture states as_of, jurisdiction, territory and forum, or expects typed clarification.
QG-0No aggregate may hide a critical failure or a failing declared slice.Release report carries per-stratum results, confidence intervals and the complete critical-event ledger.

Vocabulary

Correct means supported, applicable and materially complete. Grounded means attributable to identified official evidence; it does not by itself establish legal correctness. Useful coverage is the fraction of in-scope questions receiving a complete answer at the required risk. Abstention is a valid product outcome. A critical event is a failure whose severity blocks release regardless of the mean.

§1Governance
& posture

Pakistani legal judgment remains human; automation supplies evidence

The 2026 LJCP guidelines govern judicial institutions rather than this private product, but their human-centric, assistive, transparent, accountable and privacy-protecting posture is the right public-interest benchmark. The system therefore informs and assists; it does not substitute a model score for a Pakistani lawyer’s determination of legal effect.

Critical beats average

A wrong section that is caught and withheld contributes to abstention quality. A wrong section shown to a user is a critical event. The same rule applies to wrong province/date, fabricated authority, lost proviso, dissent-as-ratio, incorrect limitation result, cross-user disclosure and unsigned pack acceptance.

§3Evaluation

An end-to-end failure cannot say whether the source, parser, retriever, generator, legal rule or client caused it. Each layer therefore exposes a replayable evaluation port, while one orchestrator executes the same case through isolated and end-to-end tracks.

Coverage statement

A passing federal-statute benchmark authorizes only that measured scope. It does not authorize claims about complete Pakistani law, judicial invalidation, delegated legislation, a province or a court whose source coverage has not passed. The release card and client coverage banner must use the same scope registry.

§5Labels
& adjudication

The gold label is a legal argument with provenance

A single reference answer is brittle and legally dishonest. Each case stores a proposition graph: what must be said, which primary spans support it, what may also support it, what contradicts it, which qualifications are material and when the system must decline.

LegalEvalCase {
  caseId; datasetVersion; familyId; riskClass; languages[];
  question: { text; script; intendedMeaning; suppliedFacts[]; omittedFacts[] };
  scope: { jurisdiction; territory?; forum?; authorityPerspective?; asOf };
  expectedOutcome: LegalOutcomeKind;
  propositions: [{ id; text; materiality; modality; polarity;
    necessaryEvidence[]; acceptableEvidenceSets[][]; contraryEvidence[];
    qualifications[]; forbiddenClaims[] }];
  retrievalGold: { necessaryUnits[]; relevantUnits[]; hardNegatives[] };
  abstention?: { code; requiredClarification[]; unsafeAlternatives[] };
  provenance: { officialUrls[]; corpusRevision; ruleManifest?; labelledAt };
  review: { annotatorA; annotatorB; agreement; adjudicator; reasons[] };
}
StageProtocolRecorded quality
A0Case author writes facts and risk without seeing system output.Provenance, conflict declaration and issue-family key.
A1Two legal annotators independently identify operative primary sources, minimal spans, authority, time, territory and material qualifications.Raw agreement, categorical agreement and span overlap by field.
A2Senior advocate adjudicates with reasons; unresolved legitimate alternatives remain alternate gold sets.Adjudication rate and disagreement taxonomy.
A3Data engineer resolves stable legal identities and verifies source page/hash; annotator confirms rendering.100% identity and source resolution.
A4Evaluation owner assigns the entire family to one split and freezes a content digest.Leakage graph has zero cross-split edge.

Report percent agreement plus a chance-corrected statistic suitable to the field; do not worship one coefficient. Low agreement is a signal to improve guidance or preserve doctrinal ambiguity, not permission to majority-vote a contested legal proposition away. Annotators must disclose practice area, language competence and conflicts.

Measured release boundaries, 10 September 2026

nizam_clean retains all 4,595 active evidentiary revisions. Source reads release 4,594 documents; malformed document 4608 remains review-only. Migrations 0035–0040 additionally require resolved structural decisions, canonical identity, complete reconciliation of provisions promised by a printed contents list, and no unresolved internal instrument boundary. The legal views contain 3,720 instruments, 300,137 provisions and 285,156 versions; 2,312 S7 decisions and 1,988 canonical contents gaps remain blocked. Multi-expression materialisation has reduced the S10 queue to zero, confirmed independently by a source-span-aware detector.

OCR repair uses independent readings, not confidence laundering. Tesseract candidates retain word confidence and geometry; Tesseract’s official best models are a slower comparison lane. Surya / Surya-2 and PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 multilingual models provide independent Urdu-capable readings. The completed Surya batch produced 16 promoted source-derived E4 revisions, and the current audit accepts 106/106 OCR-bearing documents. That does not turn model confidence into ground truth: only a subset received direct visual checks, so later discrepancies in Urdu, citations, dates, monetary amounts and schedule cells must still be resolved against the page image or by bilingual human adjudication. The rejected page-1 candidate for document 4503 remains useful evidence of why confidence alone cannot authorize text.

Similarity is only candidate discovery. Evaluation therefore scores exact resolution, stage-one recall, reranking, deterministic legal filters, context completeness and the final decision to answer as distinct objects.

7.1Mandatory ablations

  • BM25, dense, definitions and graph independently; then RRF, cross-encoder and completeness expansion.
  • float32, halfvec(384) and bit(1024); binary ships only if Recall@50 passes and reranked NDCG loses no more than two points.
  • Approximate versus exact search by filter selectivity; shadow exact recall reported per jurisdiction and date mode.
  • Direct multilingual retrieval versus any translation proposal; compare citation-token fidelity, not just semantic score.
  • Current versus historical law, each enabled jurisdiction, precedent coverage, Roman Urdu and degraded paths.

Selective prediction

The chosen threshold is fitted only on calibration data. Publish risk against useful-answer coverage, not a single confidence number. Group-specific thresholds require enough independent calibration clusters; otherwise the conservative global threshold applies. No production incident may trigger an automatic threshold reduction.

§8Generation
& attribution

Score atomic propositions, then veto an incomplete whole

String similarity cannot determine whether an answer changed may to shall, lost an exception or cited a true but inapplicable case. Evaluation follows the AIS idea of attribution to identified sources, extended with Pakistani authority, validity and material-completeness labels.

Automatic NLI, register classifiers and LLM judges are triage signals. Before use, each is compared with lawyer labels by language, risk and proposition type; thresholds and error costs are recorded. None is the sole release authority. BLEU, ROUGE and answer-string similarity may describe wording, never legal correctness.

9.1Mutation standard

For every critical legal branch, generate mutants that invert a comparator, move a boundary by one day, remove an exception, swap a province, alter a court relation, delete a prerequisite or change may to shall. The suite must kill every critical mutant. Surviving mutants expose a missing test even when ordinary branch coverage reads 100%.

§13Statistics
& decision

Make uncertainty visible; predeclare how a candidate wins

A point estimate from 100 related questions is not proof. Release comparisons are paired by scenario cluster, use confidence intervals, preserve the incumbent baseline and separate hard safety constraints from non-inferiority and improvement claims.

Decision classRuleReporting
Hard safetyZero observed critical events and all architectural invariants pass.State sample count and the one-sided uncertainty bound; zero observed is not zero risk.
Absolute floorCandidate clears the inherited threshold overall and every declared sufficiently powered slice.Cluster bootstrap 95% interval; numerator/denominator; missing/invalid cases.
Non-inferiorityLower confidence bound for paired candidate−production delta stays above a predeclared negative margin.Margin justified before running the locked set; use paired binary tests for critical categorical outcomes.
ImprovementClaim only when the paired confidence interval excludes no change in the favourable direction.No cherry-picked best run; all preregistered primary endpoints shown.
CalibrationThreshold fixed on calibration partition; locked set estimates performance once.Reliability plot, ECE/Brier, risk–coverage and group sample sizes.

Use cluster bootstrap over base scenarios, not utterance rows. Record the family key and preserve paired failures for diagnosis. The classic rule-of-three upper bound is reported when zero failures are seen; it prevents a ten-case slice from being described as risk-free.

A release card is a signed argument for a bounded claim

Every promotion produces a human-readable card and machine-readable manifest. The card states what was measured, what was not, how uncertain the result is, which failures occurred and precisely which product scope the evidence authorizes.

19.2Definition of done

  • All five master invariants and QA-1 through QA-14 map to executable tests, named owners and evidence artifacts.
  • The 500-scenario Pakistan benchmark is source-grounded, family-split, bilingual/Roman-Urdu capable, lawyer-adjudicated and content-addressed.
  • Corpus, retrieval, oracle generation, end-to-end, domain, backend, client, offline, security and operations suites can run independently and together.
  • Critical events cannot be averaged, retried away or waived; limited scope is mechanically reflected in backend capability and client coverage.
  • Every candidate is paired with the current production baseline and reports denominators, uncertainty, slices, invalid cases, latency and cost.
  • Anonymous content is not persisted for evaluation. Production feedback enters quarantine and cannot silently contaminate locked tests or training.
  • A signed release card, rollback reference and responsible legal/technical/security approvals exist before pilot exposure.
RAuthorities
& research

Primary authority, standards and research basis

Pakistani sources establish the legal and institutional posture. International standards and research supply evaluation methods, not Pakistani law. Recent preprints are identified as supporting evidence rather than normative authority.

  1. Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan — current consolidated official text; Article 14 informs dignity and privacy risk. National Assembly official PDF.
  2. Qanun-e-Shahadat Order, 1984 — documentary and modern-device/electronic-system evidence; provenance controls do not predetermine admissibility. Pakistan Code official PDF.
  3. Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 — current Pakistan Code access copy used for security, unauthorized-access and incident-risk posture; it is not treated as a general data-retention code. Ministry of Law and Justice official PDF.
  4. Electronic Transactions Ordinance, 2002 — electronic document, communication, signature and integrity framework; a signed evaluation artifact does not by itself decide admissibility or professional approval. Pakistan Code official PDF.
  5. Pakistan Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Rules, 1976 — professional conduct including rule 146 on confidential client information. Pakistan Bar Council official PDF; official catalogue.
  6. Personal Data Protection Bill, May 2023 — the Ministry register identifies it as a draft; used for forward-compatible policy, not claimed as enacted law. MOITT official status register; official draft PDF.
  7. National Guidelines for Use of Artificial Intelligence in Judicial Institutions of Pakistan — assistive, human-centric, transparent, accountable and privacy-protecting benchmark. LJCP official guidelines PDF; official release; NJPMC adoption record.
  8. NIST AI Risk Management Framework and AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile — risk-based testing, evaluation, verification, validation, confabulation, information integrity, privacy and incident controls. NIST AI Resource Center; official profile PDF.
  9. NIST SP 800-218, Secure Software Development Framework 1.1 — secure development, provenance and vulnerability prevention practices. NIST official PDF.
  10. ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI management-system requirements and continual Plan–Do–Check–Act governance. ISO official overview.
  11. ISO/IEC 25010:2023 — current product-quality model for specifying and evaluating ICT/software quality. ISO official overview.
  12. WCAG 2.2 — testable accessibility criteria, used at AA while retaining human usability evaluation. W3C Recommendation.
  13. OWASP GenAI/LLM Top 10 2026 and API Security Top 10 2023 — prompt injection, sensitive disclosure, supply chain, overreliance, authorization and resource-consumption threats. OWASP GenAI project; OWASP API project.
  14. LegalBench-RAG — expert-annotated, fine-grained legal retrieval benchmark; methodological precedent, not a substitute for Pakistani law. Pipitone & Alami, 2024; official code.
  15. Hallucination-Free? Assessing the Reliability of Leading AI Legal Research Tools — preregistered evidence that proprietary legal RAG still produces material errors, supporting measured rather than absolute safety claims. Magesh et al., 2024.
  16. Measuring Attribution in Natural Language Generation Models — Attributable to Identified Sources (AIS) human evaluation framework. Rashkin et al., 2023.
  17. Selective Classification for Deep Neural Networks and The Art of Abstention — risk–coverage evaluation for systems that may refuse. Geifman & El-Yaniv, 2017; Xin et al., 2021.
  18. BEIR and MIRACL — heterogeneous and multilingual retrieval evaluation methodology; neither supplies Pakistani Urdu legal validation. BEIR paper; MIRACL paper.
  19. The ML Test Score — production-readiness rubric linking model, data, infrastructure and monitoring tests. Google Research publication.
  20. OpenTelemetry semantic conventions — versioned telemetry vocabulary and explicit sensitive-data caution. Official specification.

01 — Direction

Warm, not corporate

Legal software defaults to navy, gold and a white page. It reads as institutional, and institutional is exactly the feeling that keeps people from asking a legal question in the first place.

What we took

The physical artefact. Judicial stamp paper carries a cream ground, a green-blue guilloche, an embossed seal and red serial numbering — four colours that already mean something in this jurisdiction, already sit together, and already read as authoritative without shouting.

What we left

The white page. A flat white background makes every card float in nothing and every screen read as a form. Parchment gives the interface a floor, so cards can be genuinely white and actually lift.

The rule that keeps it premium

Space is the luxury signal, not ornament. Every screen in §06 could hold twice the content and deliberately does not — a section of law given room to breathe reads as considered; the same section packed tight reads as a database dump. When in doubt, remove an element rather than shrink the gaps.

03 — Type

Chrome is borrowed. Content is ours.

One decision carries the whole system. Navigation uses a face that gets out of the way; statutory text uses one with a voice, so law reads like law rather than like an app.

Aa
Spectral
Statutory text, headings, explanations. A transitional serif drawn for screens — warm, slightly narrow, and unmistakably a book face. Georgia falls back with close metrics.
Aa
Karla
Interface, labels, buttons, tab bar. A grotesque with just enough irregularity to feel drawn rather than generated. Never used for the text of a provision.
ا ب
Noto Nastaliq Urdu
Every Urdu run, chrome and content alike. There is no acceptable substitute, and no synthetic bold — emphasis comes from size and colour.

The scale, and its Nastaliq twin

Nastaliq runs larger with roughly double the leading. The two scales are chosen per run, by script — not per locale — because one sentence routinely contains both, and choosing by locale breaks on exactly that line.

The citation island

This is where bilingual legal interfaces break, and the failure does not look like a typography bug — it looks like a citation to a different section.

Without isolation

قتلِ عمد 302–304 .s PPC کے تحت

Brackets, commas and the range dash take direction from context. Inside a right-to-left paragraph they resolve right-to-left and migrate to the wrong end.

First-strong isolates

قتلِ عمد s. 302–304 PPC کے تحت

Every foreign run wrapped in U+2068 … U+2069. Neutrals resolve inside the isolate; the citation stays one island. Isolates, never the deprecated embeddings.

Digits stay Western

Section numbers, article numbers, years in citations and case numbers use 0–9 in both scripts, always. A localised section number is a wrong citation. Only quantities in running prose may take Eastern Arabic-Indic forms, and only when the interface language is Urdu.

04 — Space & form

Room is the whole point

iOS does not feel calm because of its corner radii. It feels calm because it refuses to fill the screen. Density is the single easiest way to make this product feel like a database and the hardest thing to walk back.

The seal

A ribbon-seal mark rather than a tick. It borrows from the embossed seal on the paper itself, and it is the one place a small piece of ornament earns its keep — because it is doing semantic work.

07 — Motion & tokens

Springs, never ease-in-out

Linear and cubic easing is what makes a cross-platform app feel borrowed. Everything that moves here uses a spring, and almost nothing moves.

MotionSpringApplies to
Sheet present0.40 / 0.82Provenance sheet, filters. Response over damping — it should settle, not bounce.
Push / pop0.35 / 0.90Navigation. Interruptible by the back-swipe at any point.
Pressscale 0.97Cards and buttons. 80 ms in, spring out.
Content inopacity + 8 ptAnswer blocks as they stream. Never a spinner where a skeleton fits.
Reduced motioncross-fadeEvery one of the above collapses to a 120 ms fade.

What this document is not

It fixes the vocabulary, not the screens. Screen-by-screen interaction specs for drafting and procedure belong to document 06; the offline, bilingual and accessibility rules this palette has to satisfy are in document 08 §6–7. If a future screen needs a colour that is not here, the answer is almost always a different arrangement of these, not a tenth token.

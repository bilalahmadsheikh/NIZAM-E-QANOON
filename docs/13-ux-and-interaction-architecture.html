00 — Mandate

UX is the last legal-safety boundary

A correct database record can still become a dangerous product if the screen hides its date, collapses a qualification, presents a translation as authentic text, makes abstention look like failure, or lets a user mistake possession of a phone for verified professional identity.

UX invariantVisible contractRelease proof
UX-1Every legal conclusion exposes jurisdiction, asOf, source coverage and outcome state before reliance.Users can correctly state where, when and how qualified the result is.
UX-2A citation opens the exact database-resolved provision or judgment paragraph, with material context and official-source path.Zero model-authored citation string; source-finding task succeeds across scripts.
UX-3Deadline, liberty, immediate safety and irreversible-action information precedes explanatory doctrine.Critical information is noticed and correctly restated before participants continue.
UX-4Clarification and abstention are first-class destinations; neither is rendered as an error or quietly converted to an answer.Participants understand what is missing and what safe next action exists.
UX-5English, Urdu and Roman-Urdu input preserve the same legal scope, evidence, qualifications and action consequences.Meaning-level parity gates from Doc 10 pass; translation cannot alter legal force.
UX-6Anonymous and authenticated modes make their storage consequences explicit and never imply a capability the composition lacks.Users correctly predict what will be saved, shared and deletable.
UX-7Every core journey works with keyboard/screen reader, 200% zoom, reduced motion, low-end Android and its declared offline state.WCAG 2.2 AA process audit plus real-user task completion.
UX-8Generated prose, reviewed legal rule, official text and professional identity remain visibly distinct.No tested user attributes system prose to the State, court or advocate.

Relationship to Document 12

Document 12 owns the Stamp Paper visual language—tokens, typography, spacing, component appearance, status colours and motion. Document 13 owns information order, behaviour, states, comprehension, task safety and evidence. A redesign may restyle the system without weakening either contract.

01 — Pakistan context

Design for uneven access, not a fictional average user

The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics reports for 2024–25: literacy among people aged ten and above at 63%, individual internet use at 57%, mobile/smartphone ownership at 50%, and household internet use at 70%. These aggregates do not define an individual, but they rule out a text-heavy, always-online, smartphone-expert default.

Empirical consequence

  • Start from events in ordinary language, not legal taxonomy.
  • Keep the exact law available without forcing everyone to read it first.
  • Support assisted, shared-device and low-data contexts without exposing legal matters.
  • Make offline statutory lookup a declared mode, not a broken online screen.
  • Test by province, language, gender, disability, device and digital confidence; do not infer them.

Rights and public-interest basis

The ICT Rights of Persons with Disability Act 2020 addresses accessible websites and access to justice within its statutory scope. Articles 10A, 14, 19A and 25 of the Constitution inform fair process, dignity/privacy, access to information and equality. Applicability is a legal determination; the product nevertheless adopts inclusive access nationwide as its own design commitment.

The 2026 LJCP AI Guidelines govern judicial institutions rather than this private product, but their official human-centric, assistive, transparent and accountable posture is used as a public-interest benchmark. The interface never turns model output into a judicial decision or advocate’s opinion.

Research recruits by circumstance—language, task, disability, literacy, geography, device, connectivity and professional experience—without suggesting that one characteristic predicts another. Sensitive legal scenarios use fictional facts; participants never have to disclose an active matter to test the product.

03 — Information architecture

Three doors reflect three user intentions

“Saved matters” is a workspace, not a fourth discovery mode. “Account” holds identity, sessions, privacy and language. Public pages expose only the published legal corpus. The curation console is a separate staff product and never appears as a citizen navigation branch.

05 — Question intake

Clarify only what can change the legal result

  • Never silently infer province, territory, forum or operative date from GPS, phone code, language or IP address.
  • Differentiate event dates: occurrence, notice, accrual, decision, filing and “law as at”.
  • Offer “I do not know”, approximate date and save-and-return where legally safe.
  • Repeat assumptions immediately above the answer; a material edit creates a new result, not a changed label.
  • When input contains immediate danger or a deadline, surface a bounded warning without pretending to assess emergency facts conclusively.
06 — Answer anatomy

Order information by harm, then by explanation

OrderBlockPurposeMust expose
01Outcome stateAnswer, conditional, clarify, abstain or professional review.Not a numeric confidence theatre.
02Immediate issueDeadline, liberty, safety, irreversible filing/payment.Basis, event date and uncertainty.
03Plain conclusionOne understandable proposition.Modality—may, must, cannot, depends.
04Scope cardPrevent wrong place/date/forum reliance.Jurisdiction, territory, forum, as-of, coverage.
05Material assumptionsShow which supplied/unknown facts drive the result.User fact versus system assumption.
06Law and reasoningExplain the trace without hiding exceptions.Atomic claims linked to evidence handles.
07Qualifications/conflictPreserve provisos, discretion, contrary authority and coverage gaps.Why it matters to the result.
08SourcesVerify exact primary evidence.Official path and precise cited span.

The default citizen view is progressive disclosure, not information deletion. Material qualifications are never hidden behind “read more”. The professional view increases evidence density and tools, not legal certainty. The disclaimer is structural chrome, but it cannot cure misleading answer language.

The system should sound certain only when the evidence and applicable legal rule permit certainty.

07 — Sources and status

Let the reader inspect the proof without leaving the thought

Citation chip interaction

  1. Tap/activate opens the exact stable legal unit.
  2. The matched span is highlighted without stripping provisos or hierarchy.
  3. Header shows instrument/judgment, jurisdiction, status and effective date.
  4. Reader can open official source and page coordinates.
  5. History compares applicable text; it never overwrites the current reading silently.

Judgment evidence

  • Court, date, bench and opinion type
  • Proposition actually treated
  • Majority, separate and dissenting opinions
  • Applies, distinguishes, doubts, overrules or conflict evidence
  • Coverage and last checked date
  • No “good law” badge derived from similarity or missing edges
Legal stateReader-facing languageInteraction consequence
In force for scope/date“In force as at [date] for [jurisdiction].”May support a current-law result subject to other guards.
Amended“This text changed on [date]. You are viewing the version applicable on [as-of].”Offer structured diff; answer regenerates on date change.
Repealed / invalidated / spentName the distinct legal event and effective date.Historical view only unless savings/transition makes it relevant.
Not yet commenced“Published, but commencement for this provision is not established/as appointed.”Cannot support unconditional operative-law answer.
Unknown / disputedState exactly which fact or authority is unresolved.Clarify, show competing evidence or abstain.

Changing asOf is a new legal query pinned through L3–L6; it cannot merely swap the banner. Public URLs remain stable provision identities and place the date in state/query controls without leaking private user content.

08 — Uncertainty

“We will not guess” must still move the person forward

OutcomeExplainOfferNever say
Missing material factThe fact and why it changes the branch.Answer question, mark unknown or save.“Something went wrong.”
Jurisdiction/date ambiguityCompeting scopes and consequence.Choose/confirm without inferred default.One province/date selected invisibly.
Insufficient official evidenceSources and corpus scope searched.Official-source path, report gap, professional research.Nearest result as likely answer.
Authority conflictCompeting propositions, courts, dates and coverage.Inspect both; qualified professional review.One unexplained confidence score.

An abstention retains the same calm visual hierarchy as an answer. It shows search scope, corpus version/date where useful, one primary recovery action and a report-gap path. It does not shame the question, blame the user or imply that rephrasing guarantees an answer.

09 — Language and bidi

Parity is legal force, not translated chrome

Language contracts

  • English and Urdu are equal product modes.
  • Roman Urdu is a supported input/retrieval mode, not labelled an authentic statutory text.
  • Official text is verbatim and its authentic language is labelled from L2.
  • Plain explanation is visibly separate from translation and quotation.
  • Glossary terms preserve definition scope and source.
  • “Shall”, “may”, “subject to”, “provided that” and negatives receive legal review.

Bidirectional contract

قتلِ عمد کے بارے میں دفعہ s. 302–304 PPC ملاحظہ کریں۔

Urdu blocks declare lang="ur" and dir="rtl". Citation, case number, date and URL runs are tightly wrapped LTR isolates. Western digits remain in legal identifiers. DOM order follows meaning; CSS must not reverse semantic order.

9.1Translation assurance

One grounded payload drives both languages. Independent bilingual legal reviewers label material propositions, omissions, modality, dates, actors and qualifications. A parity failure with legal effect blocks that language/version. The interface may state “translation under review” but may not silently fall back to a different legal claim.

10 — Plain language and voice

Reduce linguistic burden without reducing the law

RuleImplementationLegal guard
One propositionShort paragraph, informative heading and concrete actor/action.Do not separate a rule from its material exception.
Common words firstPlain term followed by legal term and glossary when necessary.Defined terms retain instrument-specific meaning.

ISO 24495-1:2023 and ISO 24495-2:2025 guide plain-language and legal communication, but English examples are not treated as validated Urdu rules. Pakistani users and bilingual lawyers test actual comprehension.

10.1Voice

  • Voice begins only after explicit activation; always show a live transcript that can be edited before submission.
  • Announce whether official text, translation or explanation is being read.
  • Dates, section numbers and negation receive pronunciation fixtures; provide replay, speed and text alternatives.
  • Do not infer consent or a legally consequential answer from silence, background speech or low-confidence recognition.
  • If anonymous voice cannot remain request-local under Doc 11, it is unavailable with a plain explanation—not secretly persisted.
11 — Calculators and drafting

Deterministic tools show their work

Deadline and money calculation

  1. Name jurisdiction, remedy and rule version.
  2. Collect each legally distinct event date.
  3. Show included/excluded days, holidays and branch decisions.
  4. Render result as date, range or unknown—not false precision.
  5. Confirm inputs before export; attach sources and trace.
  6. Explain that court findings/discretion may change applicability.

Document drafting

  1. Separate supplied facts, assumptions and unresolved fields.
  2. Reviewed rules—not the model—select approved clauses.
  3. Trace each clause to rule, version and official source.
  4. Mark generated text and draft/review state on every page.
  5. Prevent export when mandatory facts or safety gates fail.
  6. No “lawyer reviewed” status without named valid review event.

WCAG 2.2’s error-prevention requirements for legal, financial and data actions inform confirmation, review and reversibility. Submission to a court or authority is outside the pilot unless separately designed, authorised, integrated and legally reviewed.

12 — Trust and privacy

Make data consequences legible at the moment of choice

  • Anonymous/signed-in state is named near the question field and history control, not buried in policy text.
  • Notification previews and screen titles are privacy-neutral by default; user can choose a private matter label.
  • Share/export names recipient/scope/expiry and offers revocation where supported. No public bearer link by default.
  • Account privacy centre shows categories, sessions, exports, deletion queue and optional-reuse consent independently.
  • Security cannot be marketed as advocate-client privilege. Professional confidentiality belongs to applicable legal relationships, not interface decoration.

Status updates use programmatically determinable messages without stealing focus. Errors preserve entered values except secrets, attach messages to fields, describe recovery and never expose traces, identifiers or legal content in support codes.

14 — Accessibility

AA is the floor; task equivalence is the outcome

Core web journeys target WCAG 2.2 AA as complete processes. Native mobile uses the same success criteria where applicable plus platform semantics and Document 08’s 48 dp target. A conformance claim requires declared scope, versions and evidence; automated scans alone cannot make it.

CapabilityAcceptance contract
Structure/navigationOne logical heading hierarchy, landmarks, skip link, meaningful titles, stable help and visible location.
Keyboard/switchAll actions reachable; no trap; visible unobscured focus; no drag-only action; reading and focus order match.
Screen readerNames/roles/states, table relationships, expanded/collapsed source state, status announcements and streaming batches tested.
VisualText reflows at 320 CSS px and 200% zoom; contrast and non-colour status marks; no clipped Nastaliq or forced orientation.
Motor/touch48 dp product target with clear spacing; cancellation/reversal for high-impact action; no precision gesture requirement.
Cognitive/languageConsistent navigation, plain instructions, redundant-entry avoidance, review before legal/data action and no timed reading.
Audio/speechTranscript/caption alternative, no autoplay, microphone state announced and input does not rely on pronunciation alone.

WCAG does not cover every cognitive, language or learning need. The W3C supplemental cognitive guidance and testing with real disabled users therefore sit alongside—not underneath—conformance testing. Urdu screen-reader, font scaling, bidi focus order and mixed-script pronunciation receive dedicated fixtures.

15 — Mobile and offline

Connectivity is a capability state, not a moral judgment

Full online

Retrieval, verified generation, domain tools, authenticated matters and sync. Optimistic chrome never fabricates legal completion.

Degraded online

Deterministic citation/public corpus continues. Provider-dependent answer is clearly unavailable; queued writes are idempotent and user-visible.

Offline

Signed held statutes, local exact/lexical search, bookmarks and precomputed explainers. No implication that pack scope equals all Pakistani law.

  • Pack header shows corpus revision, jurisdictions/source kinds, publication date, signature state and update size before download.
  • “Not in this pack”, “not found in held law” and “no applicable law established” are distinct outcomes.
  • Install is atomic; tampered/incompatible pack is rejected; last-known-good remains usable and rollback is explained.
  • Reconnect compares local intentions and server state; conflicts are presented by meaning, never resolved last-write-wins for legal artefacts.
  • Performance budgets from Doc 08 protect usable first content, scrolling and input on representative low-end hardware.

Report task success, critical errors, unassisted completion, comprehension, time where appropriate, confidence calibration and qualitative cause by declared slice. Satisfaction never compensates for a legal-safety failure. Doc 10 controls sampling, confidence intervals, critical-event vetoes, bilingual parity and release cards.

UX release blockers

Any tested path that makes repealed law appear current; hides a material deadline; fabricates or prevents verification of a citation; changes legal meaning in Urdu; turns abstention into an answer/error; implies phone possession is identity; exposes another matter; claims anonymous persistence; accepts a tampered pack; or permits an unreviewed legal action is an S0/S1 event.

19.1Definition of ready

A feature is not ready for implementation until its journey, all failure states, content, data consequence, API contract, accessibility semantics, offline behaviour, legal review points, analytics allowlist and acceptance tests are present. A static happy-path mock is insufficient.

19.2Build order

  1. Research foundation and high-harm journey scenarios.
  2. Information architecture, anonymous question, exact citation and abstention.
  3. Source viewer, temporal status and English/Urdu parity.
  4. Authenticated matters, privacy controls and changed-law state.
  5. Deterministic calculators/procedures, then reviewed drafting.
  6. Professional research and curation workspaces.
  7. Continuous accessibility, low-device, offline and comprehension assurance.

How to transcribe a page (read this first)
  • Type what the page prints, nothing else. Keep old spellings and printer's errors (committted stays). Do not fill words in from another copy of the Act.
  • A word you cannot read, or that is lost in a tear, stain or binding: write [illegible] in its place.
  • One paragraph per line group, blank line between paragraphs, in reading order. On two-column pages finish the left column before the right.
  • Put a marginal note on its own line starting Side note: and a footnote on its own line starting Footnote:.
  • Leave out the running head, page number and printer's imprint.
  • Tables: one row per line, cells separated by | , keep every figure exactly as printed. A figure you cannot read is [illegible], never a guess.
  • The box starts with the machine OCR text. It is wrong in places (dropped words, wrong digits, rows out of order, running heads mixed in). Read it against the image word by word before you mark a page checked. Use "Blank" and type the page if the OCR is far off.
  • Status: Checked = matches the page; Checked, has [illegible] = you marked unreadable words; Duplicate scan = the same page as an earlier scan (say which in the note); No law text = cover, blank or imprint page.
